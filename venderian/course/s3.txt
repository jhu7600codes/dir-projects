section 3 | talking for real | feelings, stories, hobbies and big ideas

module 7 | feelings and health

lesson feelings
new: senta
+ angra | angry | adjective
+ timë | afraid, scared | adjective
+ seren | calm | adjective
mi fela senta. = I feel happy.
tu kwa tris? = why are you sad?
aba angra. = father is angry.
kiti un noch timë. = the child is scared at night.
ama seren. = mother is calm.

lesson because
+ kaus | because | conjunction
tip: kaus (because) starts a second part of the sentence. every part keeps its verb at the end.
mi tris, kaus vren da mi dalë. = I am sad because my friend is far away.
sa fela, kaus sa dara ha. = she is happy because she has a gift.
min ne golta, kaus pluvata. = we did not go because it rained.
tu kwa angra? = why are you angry?
kaus mi ludo perdata. = because I lost the game.

lesson the body
new: kop, vis, mun, kor
+ pedë | foot, leg | noun
+ braso | arm | noun
+ naso | nose | noun
+ dola | to hurt | verb
kop da mi dola. = my head hurts.
mi dor braso i dor pedë ha. = I have two arms and two legs.
kiti visan bela ha. = the child has beautiful eyes.
pedë da tu dola ka? = does your leg hurt?
naso da vuf frid. = the dog's nose is cold.

lesson at the doctor
+ sika | sick | adjective
+ pilë | pill, medicine | noun
+ sana | healthy, well | adjective
+ resta | to stay | verb
mi sika. = I am sick.
medik mi ayda. = the doctor helps me.
pilë yama! = take the medicine!
posdey tu sana esva. = tomorrow you will be well.
mi un kama resta deba. = I have to stay in bed.

lesson if
+ sef | if | conjunction
tip: sef (if) works like kaus: the if part comes first and ends with its verb, then the rest.
sef tu sika, ra medik gol. = if you are sick, go to the doctor.
sef pluva, min un kar resta. = if it rains, we stay at home.
sef mi kron ha, mi tu aydava. = if I have time, I will help you.
sef sol shina, min ra pelag golva. = if the sun shines, we will go to the sea.
sef tu fela, mi fela. = if you are happy, I am happy.

module 7.5 | friends and stories

lesson friends
+ fida | to trust | verb
+ rida | to laugh | verb
vren bonesh da mi ana. = my best friend is Ana.
min kun ridata. = we laughed together.
mi vren da mi fida. = I trust my friend.
tu vrenan multa ha ka? = do you have many friends?
vrenan bon ayda. = good friends help.

lesson when
tip: kwan is also "when" in the middle of a sentence: the when part comes first.
kwan mi pik esta, mi un ferma vivata. = when I was small, I lived on a farm.
kwan tu venva, min kun yamava. = when you come, we will eat together.
kwan pluva, mish un kar resta. = when it rains, the cat stays at home.
kwan sa fela, sa lira. = when she is happy, she sings.
kwan dedo ven, kitin fela. = when grandfather comes, the children are happy.

lesson once upon a time
+ yordo | long ago | adverb
+ rey | king | noun
+ kastë | castle | noun
+ drako | dragon | noun
yordo, ek rey un kastë vivata. = long ago, a king lived in a castle.
drako gran ra kastë venta. = a big dragon came to the castle.
rey timë esta. = the king was scared.
drako rey ne yamata. = the dragon did not eat the king.
drako i rey vrenan esta! = the dragon and the king were friends!

lesson who and that
+ ki | who, which, that (describes the noun before it) | particle
tip: ki starts a description of the noun before it, and the description ends with its own verb: "hum ki ik viva", the person who lives here.
hum ki ik viva bon. = the person who lives here is good.
mi vren ki vendëri thela ha. = I have a friend who speaks Venderian.
kitë ki tu predey kopata mol bon. = the book that you bought yesterday is very good.
drako ki un bor viva gran. = the dragon that lives on the mountain is big.
hum ki mi aydata medik. = the person who helped me is a doctor.

lesson the end of the story
+ sempo | always | adverb
un fin, tot felamo vivata. = in the end, everyone lived happily.
ve sago bonesh. = this story is the best.
ve sago ripë thela! = tell this story again!
mi sagon ki drakon ha goda. = I like stories that have dragons.
kitin sago tot otata. = the children heard the whole story.

module 8 | hobbies and music

lesson hobbies
+ hobi | hobby | noun
+ dansa | to dance | verb
+ pinta | to draw, to paint | verb
+ gitar | guitar | noun
hobi da tu kwo? = what is your hobby?
mi pinta goda. = I like to draw.
sa gitar luda. = she plays the guitar.
min un festa dansata. = we danced at the party.
tu dansa pot ka? = can you dance?

lesson music
+ bandë | band | noun
+ shoa | show, concert | noun
+ voka | voice | noun
mi muzik ota goda. = I like to listen to music.
bandë ve vechë luda. = the band plays this evening.
lirari voka bela ha. = the singer has a beautiful voice.
min ra shoa golva. = we will go to the concert.
ve lir mol fela. = this song is very happy.

lesson I think that
new: kenë
+ ke | that (I think that...) | conjunction
tip: ke is the one thing that comes after the verb: "mi penka ke ...", I think that ... the part after ke has its own verb at the end.
mi penka ke muzik bon. = I think that music is good.
sa thelata ke sa posdey venva. = she said that she will come tomorrow.
mi kenë ke tu gitar luda pot. = I know that you can play the guitar.
tu penka ke ve lir bela ka? = do you think that this song is beautiful?
min otata ke bandë nov. = we heard that the band is new.

lesson sports
+ sporta | sport | noun
+ bal | ball | noun
+ ekipa | team | noun
+ vina | to win | verb
tu sporta goda ka? = do you like sports?
kitin bal luda. = the children play ball.
ekipa da min vinata! = our team won!
ekipa da san perdata. = their team lost.
posdey min vinava. = tomorrow we will win.

lesson games
+ regla | rule | noun
+ vez | turn (in a game) | noun
reglan da ludo kwo? = what are the rules of the game?
vez da tu! = it is your turn!
ludari bonesh vina. = the best player wins.
ve ludo lekhor dë vo. = this game is easier than that one.
mi ludo ripë luda vola. = I want to play the game again.

module 8.5 | games and sport

lesson at the stadium
+ stadyo | stadium | noun
+ fanu | fan (of a team) | noun
+ krida | to shout | verb
min un stadyo esta. = we were at the stadium.
fanun multa kridata. = many fans shouted.
ekipa da mi rapmo kurata. = my team ran fast.
tu stadyo goda ka? = do you like the stadium?
un fin, fanun fela esta. = in the end, the fans were happy.

lesson training
+ trena | to train, to practice | verb
mi dey tot trena. = I train every day.
sa forër dë mi. = she is stronger than me.
trena khar, shom bon. = training is hard but good.
sef tu trena, tu vinava. = if you train, you will win.
medik thelata ke mi trena deba. = the doctor said that I have to train.

lesson board games
new: kwi
+ shakh | chess | noun
+ karta | card | noun
+ kubo | dice | noun
+ lansa | to throw | verb
dedo shakh bonmo luda. = grandfather plays chess well.
min kartan luda. = we play cards.
kubo lansa! = throw the dice!
shakh ne lekh. = chess is not easy.
kwi vinata? = who won?

lesson winning and losing
+ ekwa | fair | adjective
vinari mol fela esta. = the winner was very happy.
perda ne mal. = losing is not bad.
ludo ekwa esta. = the game was fair.
kwan tu perda, tu lerna. = when you lose, you learn.
min kun vinata. = we won together.

lesson the big game
vedey ludo gran es. = today there is a big game.
ekipa da mi trenata i trenata. = my team trained and trained.
mi penka ke min vinava. = I think that we will win.
un fin, ekipa da min vinata! = in the end, our team won!
ve dey bonesh da yer esta. = this was the best day of the year.

module 9 | ideas and opinions

lesson opinions
new: ver, fal
+ ideya | idea | noun
+ kreda | to believe | verb
ve ideya bon. = this idea is good.
mi kreda ke ve ver. = I believe that this is true.
tu kwo penka? = what do you think?
sa thelata ke ve fal. = she said that this is false.
ideya da tu mol bon. = your idea is very good.

lesson agreeing
+ konsa | to agree | verb
+ rasa | reason | noun
+ mayë | maybe | adverb
mi kel tu konsa. = I agree with you.
mi ne konsa. = I do not agree.
mayë tu ver. = maybe you are right.
rasa da tu kwo? = what is your reason?
mi konsa, kaus ve ideya bon. = I agree because this idea is good.

lesson big words
new: -tal
+ vala | important | adjective
tip: add -tal to an adjective to make it a thing: fela (happy), felatal (happiness).
felatal mol vala. = happiness is very important.
vertal sempo bon. = the truth is always good.
libërtal vala. = freedom is important.
mi belatal da pelag liva. = I love the beauty of the sea.
bontal un kor viva. = goodness lives in the heart.

lesson the world
+ mundo | world | noun
+ pazo | peace | noun
+ gera | war | noun
+ avenë | future | noun
mundo gran i bela. = the world is big and beautiful.
human tot pazo vola. = all people want peace.
gera mal. = war is bad.
avenë da mundo un khanan da min es. = the future of the world is in our hands.
mi mundo tot vaya vola. = I want to travel the whole world.

lesson languages
new: thelor, thel
+ inglish | English (the language) | name
+ rusë | Russian (the language) | name
+ signa | to mean | verb
mi tis thelor thela. = I speak three languages.
vendëri lekhor dë inglish. = Venderian is easier than English.
tu rusë thela pot ka? = can you speak Russian?
ve thel kwo signa? = what does this word mean?
lerna thelor nov khar, shom bon. = learning a new language is hard but good.

module 9.5 | big conversations

lesson dreams
+ sonyo | dream | noun
+ sonya | to dream | verb
+ fama | famous | adjective
mi sonyo gran ha. = I have a big dream.
un noch, mi sonyata ke mi alata. = at night, I dreamed that I flew.
sonyo da tu kwo? = what is your dream?
mi lirari fama es vola. = I want to be a famous singer.
sef tu kreda, sonyo da tu ver esva. = if you believe, your dream will come true.

lesson memories
+ memo | memory | noun
+ rekorda | to remember | verb
+ oblida | to forget | verb
mi dey eki da skol rekorda. = I remember the first day of school.
mi memon bela multa ha. = I have many beautiful memories.
tu ishe da mi oblidata ka? = did you forget my name?
kwan mi kiti esta, mi un noch timë esta. = when I was a child, I was scared at night.
memo da dedo sempo un kor da mi es. = the memory of grandfather is always in my heart.

lesson the future
+ muda | to change | verb
mundo muda. = the world is changing.
un avenë, human sur mera vivava. = in the future, people will live on the moon.
mi penka ke avenë bon esva. = I think that the future will be good.
tu un avenë kwo fasava? = what will you do in the future?
mi muda pot. = I can change.

lesson a real conversation
vela! tu kwu? = hello! how are you?
mi bon, shevan. i tu? = I am good, thanks. and you?
mi mol fela, kaus mi vendëri lerna. = I am very happy because I am learning Venderian.
tu bonmo thela! = you speak well!
shevan! mi dey tot trena. = thanks! I practice every day.

lesson almost there
nun tu vendëri thela pot. = now you can speak Venderian.
tu multamo lernata. = you learned a lot.
dey tot trena, i ne oblida! = practice every day, and do not forget!
vendëri thelor belash. = Venderian is the most beautiful language.
dano, vren! = goodbye, friend!
