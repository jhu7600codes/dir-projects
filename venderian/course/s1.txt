section 1 | first steps | family, food and home

module 1 | family!

lesson hello
new: vela, dano, ya, nay, mi, tu, ka
+ ana | Ana (a name) | name
+ tomo | Tomo (a name) | name
tip: there is no word for am / is / are in the present. "mi ana" just means "I am Ana". ka at the very end turns a sentence into a yes/no question.
vela! = hello!
vela, ana! = hello, Ana!
mi ana. = I am Ana.
tu tomo ka? = are you Tomo?
ya, mi tomo. = yes, I am Tomo.
nay, mi ana. = no, I am Ana.
dano, tomo! = goodbye, Tomo!

lesson mother and father
new: ama, aba, da, ve, vo
tip: da means "of", and it comes after the thing: "ama da mi" is "mother of me", so "my mother".
ve ama da mi. = this is my mother.
vo aba da mi. = that is my father.
ve aba da tu ka? = is this your father?
ya, ve aba da mi. = yes, this is my father.
vo ama da ana. = that is Ana's mother.

lesson brothers and sisters
new: sa, ha, i, kiti
+ bro | brother | noun
+ sesa | sister | noun
tip: the verb always goes last. "mi bro ha" is "I brother have". sa means he, she and it.
mi bro i sesa ha. = I have a brother and a sister.
sa sesa da tomo. = she is Tomo's sister.
sa kiti. = he is a child.
tu sesa ha ka? = do you have a sister?
nay, mi bro ha. = no, I have a brother.

lesson more than one
new: -n, -an, min, tun, san, dor, tis
tip: plural: add -n after a vowel (kiti, kitin) and -an after a consonant. after a number the noun stays as it is: dor bro, two brothers.
mi dor bro ha. = I have two brothers.
san kitin. = they are children.
min sesan. = we are sisters.
tun bron ka? = are you brothers?
sa tis kiti ha. = she has three children.

lesson my family
new: folka, ek, gran, pik, vet
+ baba | grandmother | noun
+ dedo | grandfather | noun
tip: adjectives come after the noun. with no verb they make the whole sentence: "folka gran" is "the family is big".
folka da mi gran. = my family is big.
mi ek sesa ha. = I have one sister.
baba i dedo vet. = grandmother and grandfather are old.
bro da mi pik. = my brother is small.
folka da tu pik ka? = is your family small?

module 1.5 | family traditions

lesson at home
new: kar, un, viva, ik, lë, kwe
tip: little words like un (in) go before the noun: "un kar", in the house. question words sit where the answer goes.
mi un kar viva. = I live in a house.
baba ik viva. = grandmother lives here.
min un kar gran viva. = we live in a big house.
tu kwe viva? = where do you live?
san lë viva. = they live there.

lesson dinner together
new: yama, sipa, yamë, nami, tot, ne
+ vespa | dinner | noun
tip: ne (not) goes right before the verb. tot after a noun means all of it: "folka tot", the whole family.
folka da mi vespa yama. = my family eats dinner.
folka tot vespa yama. = the whole family eats dinner.
aba nami sipa. = father drinks water.
kiti yamë ne yama. = the child does not eat the food.
tu nami sipa ka? = do you drink water?

lesson holidays
new: do, ra, fela, liva
+ festa | party, holiday | noun
+ dara | gift | noun
tip: ra means "to": you give something ra someone.
ama dara ra mi do. = mother gives a gift to me.
mi dara ra baba do. = I give a gift to grandmother.
tot fela. = everyone is happy.
min festan liva. = we love parties.
kiti fela ka? = is the child happy?

lesson old stories
new: thela, ota, multa
+ sago | story | noun
+ vechë | evening | noun
dedo sago thela. = grandfather tells a story.
kitin sago ota. = the children hear the story.
un vechë, baba sagon thela. = in the evening, grandmother tells stories.
dedo sagon multa ha. = grandfather has many stories.
tu sago ota ka? = do you hear the story?

lesson we love each other
new: ishe, goda, vren, bon, mol
+ kun | together | adverb
tip: mol (very) goes right before the word it makes stronger.
ishe da mi ana. = my name is Ana.
min kun yama. = we eat together.
folka da mi mol bon. = my family is very good.
mi vren da tu goda. = I like your friend.
tu folka da tu liva ka? = do you love your family?

module 2 | food

lesson hungry
new: bren, mikh, vola, kwo
+ kafa | coffee | noun
+ chay | tea | noun
mi bren vola. = I want bread.
tu kwo vola? = what do you want?
sa mikh sipa. = he drinks milk.
min chay vola. = we want tea.
aba kafa sipa ka? = does father drink coffee?

lesson fruit
new: rud, gel, verdë
+ aplo | apple | noun
+ banan | banana | noun
+ frut | fruit | noun
+ sava | sweet | adjective
aplo rud. = the apple is red.
banan gel. = the banana is yellow.
frut sava. = the fruit is sweet.
mi aplo verdë goda. = I like the green apple.
tu bananan goda ka? = do you like bananas?

lesson numbers
new: kav, pam, shel, sib, okh, nir, dek, zen, es
tip: es (to be) also means "there is / there are". numbers go before the noun.
ik pam banan es. = there are five bananas here.
lë dek aplo es. = there are ten apples there.
dor i tis pam. = two and three is five.
baba okh kiti ha. = grandmother has eight children.
min sib frut yama. = we eat seven fruits.

lesson at the market
new: kopa, zil
+ bazar | market | noun
+ kosta | to cost | verb
+ kwanto | how much, how many | adverb
+ mon | coin | noun
mi un bazar es. = I am at the market.
mi aplon kopa. = I buy apples.
aplo kwanto kosta? = how much does the apple cost?
aplo dor mon kosta. = the apple costs two coins.
nay, mi zil ne ha. = no, I do not have money.

lesson cooking
new: fasa, kal, frid
+ kuka | to cook | verb
+ supa | soup | noun
+ salë | salt | noun
ama supa kuka. = mother cooks soup.
supa kal. = the soup is hot.
nami frid. = the water is cold.
aba bren fasa. = father makes bread.
supa salë ne ha. = the soup has no salt.

module 2.5 | cooking and meals

lesson breakfast
new: o
+ matin | morning | noun
+ pran | breakfast | noun
+ ovo | egg | noun
un matin, mi pran yama. = in the morning, I eat breakfast.
mi dor ovo yama. = I eat two eggs.
tu kafa o chay vola? = do you want coffee or tea?
kitin mikh sipa. = the children drink milk.
pran bon. = breakfast is good.

lesson in the kitchen
new: sur
+ kukin | kitchen | noun
+ tabla | table | noun
+ nash | knife | noun
+ plato | plate | noun
plato sur tabla es. = the plate is on the table.
nash un kukin es. = the knife is in the kitchen.
ama un kukin kuka. = mother cooks in the kitchen.
platon kwe es? = where are the plates?
platon sur tabla. = the plates are on the table.

lesson tastes
new: mal
+ amar | bitter | adjective
+ salo | salty | adjective
+ gusta | to taste | verb
kafa amar. = coffee is bitter.
supa mol salo. = the soup is very salty.
mi supa gusta. = I taste the soup.
frut sava i bon. = the fruit is sweet and good.
ve yamë mal ka? = is this food bad?

lesson please and thank you
new: kiro, shevan
tip: to tell someone to do something, leave out tu: just say the object and the verb. put kiro (please) in front to be nice.
kiro, nami ra mi do. = please give water to me.
shevan! = thank you!
kiro, bren ra baba do. = please give bread to grandmother.
supa yama! = eat the soup!
kafa ne sipa! = do not drink the coffee!

lesson a family dinner
+ yamik | hungry | adjective
+ satë | full (not hungry) | adjective
mi yamik. = I am hungry.
tu yamik ka? = are you hungry?
nay, mi satë. = no, I am full.
ama torta fasa. = mother makes a cake.
folka tot torta goda. = the whole family likes the cake.

module 3 | home

lesson rooms
new: dur, vindë, bela
+ rum | room | noun
+ kama | bed | noun
+ stul | chair | noun
kar da mi tis rum ha. = my house has three rooms.
kama un rum es. = the bed is in the room.
stul pik. = the chair is small.
dur kwe es? = where is the door?
vindë gran i bela. = the window is big and beautiful.

lesson colors
new: blu, alba, mog, mish, vuf
+ gris | gray | adjective
+ roza | pink | adjective
dur blu. = the door is blue.
kama da mi alba. = my bed is white.
stul mog. = the chair is black.
mish gris sur kama es. = the gray cat is on the bed.
vuf da mi ne roza. = my dog is not pink.

lesson doing things
new: apra, klu, seda, sta, nema
dur apra! = open the door!
kiro, vindë klu. = please close the window.
mi sur stul seda. = I sit on the chair.
kiti un kama nema. = the child sleeps in the bed.
vuf ne seda. = the dog does not sit.

lesson where is it?
new: tal, kitë
+ nër | under | preposition
+ nek | next to | preposition
+ ante | in front of, before | preposition
+ pos | behind | preposition
vuf nër tabla es. = the dog is under the table.
stul nek kama es. = the chair is next to the bed.
mish pos dur es. = the cat is behind the door.
kar ante tal es. = the house is in front of the tree.
kitë kwe es? = where is the book?

lesson my room
new: shom, lum
+ lampa | lamp | noun
+ kloza | closet | noun
tip: with no verb, ne goes before the adjective: "lum ne bon", the light is not good.
rum da mi pik shom bela. = my room is small but beautiful.
lampa sur tabla es. = the lamp is on the table.
lum ne bon. = the light is not good.
kitën multa un kloza es. = there are many books in the closet.
mi rum da mi liva. = I love my room.

module 3.5 | daily routine

lesson morning
new: khan, gol, kwan
+ leva | to get up | verb
+ lava | to wash | verb
+ dentë | tooth | noun
mi un matin leva. = I get up in the morning.
mi khanan lava. = I wash my hands.
kiti dentën lava. = the child washes their teeth.
tu kwan leva? = when do you get up?
aba kafa sipa i gol. = father drinks coffee and goes.

lesson time words
new: nun, vedey, posdey, predey, dey, noch, tris
nun mi yamik. = now I am hungry.
vedey dey bon. = today is a good day.
posdey min ra baba gol. = tomorrow we go to grandmother.
un noch, mi nema. = at night, I sleep.
predey ama tris. = yesterday mother was sad.

lesson at school
new: skol, lerna, ridë, skri
+ mestro | teacher | noun
+ vendëri | Venderian (the language) | name
mi un skol lerna. = I learn at school.
mestro kitë ridë. = the teacher reads a book.
kitin skri. = the children write.
tu vendëri lerna ka? = are you learning Venderian?
ya, mi vendëri lerna. = yes, I am learning Venderian.

lesson evening
new: luda, lira, lir, ludo
+ bano | bath | noun
tip: want, like and can go after the other verb: "mi nema vola" is "I want to sleep".
un vechë, min ludo luda. = in the evening, we play a game.
ama lir lira. = mother sings a song.
kiti un bano es. = the child is in the bath.
tu ludon goda ka? = do you like games?
mi nema vola. = I want to sleep.

lesson my day
new: rabo, rab, kwu
+ apre | after | preposition
+ fin | end | noun
aba un dey rabo. = father works during the day.
apre skol, mi ludo luda. = after school, I play a game.
ante vespa, min khanan lava. = before dinner, we wash our hands.
un fin da dey, mi nema. = at the end of the day, I sleep.
dey da tu kwu? = how is your day?
