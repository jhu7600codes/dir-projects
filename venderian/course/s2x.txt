# section 2, lessons 6-10 of every module

section 2 | out in the world | the city, nature, school and work

module 4 | city and travel

lesson the bus
+ bus | bus | noun
+ stasyo | stop, station | noun
+ bilet | ticket | noun
bus un stasyo es. = the bus is at the stop.
mi bilet kopata. = I bought a ticket.
bus tarda. = the bus is late.
tu kel bus golva ka? = will you go by bus?
stasyo kwe es? = where is the station?

lesson the airport
+ avyon | airplane | noun
+ avyokar | airport | noun
avyon mol rap. = the airplane is very fast.
min ra avyokar golta. = we went to the airport.
avyon gran i alba. = the airplane is big and white.
mi bagë gran ha. = I have a big bag.
posdey min kel avyon vayava. = tomorrow we will travel by airplane.

lesson the hotel room
+ klavë | key | noun
rum da min un hotel pik. = our room in the hotel is small.
klavë da rum kwe es? = where is the room key?
mi klavë ne ha. = I do not have the key.
rum vindë gran ha. = the room has a big window.
min ik dor noch esva. = we will be here for two nights.

lesson prices
+ karo | expensive | adjective
+ barat | cheap | adjective
hotel karo. = the hotel is expensive.
bus barat. = the bus is cheap.
ve bilet kwanto kosta? = how much does this ticket cost?
avyon karo, tren barat. = the airplane is expensive, the train is cheap.
mi zil multa ne ha. = I do not have a lot of money.

lesson postcards
+ posta | postcard | noun
+ manda | to send | verb
mi posta ra baba mandata. = I sent a postcard to grandmother.
posta da tu bela. = your postcard is beautiful.
mi postan multa kopata. = I bought many postcards.
tu posta ra mi mandava ka? = will you send me a postcard?
baba posta ridëta. = grandmother read the postcard.

module 4.5 | directions

lesson the bridge
new: ruma
+ ponto | bridge | noun
ponto sur ruma es. = the bridge is over the river.
min sur ponto pasata. = we walked over the bridge.
ponto vet i bela. = the bridge is old and beautiful.
ruma gran nër ponto es. = a big river is under the bridge.
ponto kwe es? = where is the bridge?

lesson the town square
+ plaza | square (in a town) | noun
+ sentro | center | noun
+ fontan | fountain | noun
+ turë | tower | noun
plaza un sentro da urba es. = the square is in the center of the city.
fontan un plaza es. = there is a fountain in the square.
turë mol gran. = the tower is very big.
min ra turë golva. = we will go to the tower.
sentro prok ka? = is the center near?

lesson taking a taxi
new: dar
+ taksi | taxi | noun
+ kondu | to drive | verb
taksi rap. = the taxi is fast.
dar taksi kondu. = the man drives the taxi.
min kel taksi ra hotel golta. = we went to the hotel by taxi.
taksi karo ka? = is the taxi expensive?
aba oto kondu. = father drives the car.

lesson souvenirs
+ suvenir | souvenir | noun
mi suvenir ra sesa kopata. = I bought a souvenir for my sister.
suvenir pik i bela. = the souvenir is small and beautiful.
boteka suveniran multa ha. = the shop has many souvenirs.
ve suvenir kwanto kosta? = how much does this souvenir cost?
suvenir barat. = the souvenir is cheap.

lesson coming home
+ torna | to come back | verb
min ra kar tornata. = we came back home.
vuf mol fela esta. = the dog was very happy.
kar da min mol bon. = our home is very good.
mi nema vola. = I want to sleep.
posdey mi foton ra vren mandava. = tomorrow I will send the photos to my friend.

module 5 | nature and weather

lesson the beach
+ playa | beach | noun
+ sablo | sand | noun
+ onda | wave | noun
playa bela. = the beach is beautiful.
sablo kal. = the sand is hot.
ondan gran. = the waves are big.
kitin sur playa ludata. = the children played on the beach.
pelag blu. = the sea is blue.

lesson storms
+ storma | storm | noun
+ tondra | thunder | noun
vedey storma esva. = today there will be a storm.
tondra forë. = the thunder is strong.
mish tondra ne goda. = the cat does not like thunder.
un storma, min un kar es. = during the storm, we are at home.
storma ripë venta. = the storm came again.

lesson plants
+ planta | plant | noun
+ rado | root | noun
planta nami vola. = the plant wants water.
talan radon lon ha. = trees have long roots.
mi planta pik un rum ha. = I have a small plant in my room.
plantan verdë. = the plants are green.
ama plantan nami do. = mother gives water to the plants.

lesson insects
+ insek | insect | noun
+ abë | bee | noun
+ mosko | fly (the insect) | noun
+ papiyon | butterfly | noun
abë mel fasa. = the bee makes honey.
papiyon bela. = the butterfly is beautiful.
insekan multa un gardo es. = there are many insects in the garden.
mosko mal. = the fly is bad.
kiti abën ne goda. = the child does not like bees.

lesson hot and cold
vedey kalor dë predey. = today is hotter than yesterday.
noch fridor dë dey. = the night is colder than the day.
nami un lago frid. = the water in the lake is cold.
ve dey kalesh da yer. = this day is the hottest of the year.
sol kalesh. = the sun is the hottest.

lesson climbing a mountain

lesson the night sky
+ planet | planet | noun
+ kosmo | space (beyond the sky) | noun

mi ra kosmo vaya vola. = I want to travel to space.

lesson flowers

module 6 | school and work

lesson the break
+ pauza | break | noun
+ kantina | cafeteria | noun
un pauza, min luda. = during the break, we play.
pauza dek minut. = the break is ten minutes.
min un kantina yama. = we eat in the cafeteria.
yamë da kantina ne bon. = the food in the cafeteria is not good.
pauza kwan? = when is the break?

lesson grades
+ nota | grade | noun
mi nota bon ha. = I have a good grade.
nota da tu bonor dë nota da mi. = your grade is better than my grade.
mestro notan dota. = the teacher gave grades.
sesa notan bonesh ha. = my sister has the best grades.
mi nota mal ha, shom mi lerna. = I have a bad grade, but I learn.

lesson computers
+ kompu | computer | noun
+ ekran | screen | noun
+ tipa | to type | verb
mi kompu nov ha. = I have a new computer.
ekran gran. = the screen is big.
mi tasko kel kompu fasa. = I do homework with the computer.
aba rapmo tipa. = father types quickly.
kompu da skol vet. = the school's computer is old.

lesson the library
+ biblio | library | noun
+ silen | quiet | adjective
biblio kitën multa ha. = the library has many books.
biblio silen. = the library is quiet.
mi kitë un biblio ridë. = I read a book in the library.
tu biblio goda ka? = do you like the library?
min un biblio silenmo thela. = we speak quietly in the library.

lesson questions and answers
+ kwesto | question | noun
+ repo | answer | noun
mi kwesto ha. = I have a question.
kwesto da tu bon. = your question is good.
mestro kwesto da mi repata. = the teacher answered my question.
kitin kweston multa ha. = the children have many questions.
tu repo kenë ka? = do you know the answer?

module 6.5 | time and plans

lesson dream jobs
+ piloto | pilot | noun
+ teknik | engineer | noun
mi piloto es vola. = I want to be a pilot.
sesa da mi teknik esva. = my sister will be an engineer.
piloto avyon kondu. = the pilot flies the airplane.
rab da tu kwo esva? = what will your job be?
mi lirari es vola. = I want to be a singer.

lesson the office
+ ofis | office | noun
+ shef | boss | noun
aba un ofis rabo. = father works in an office.
shef da aba bon. = father's boss is good.
ofis gran i silen. = the office is big and quiet.
ama un ofis ne rabo. = mother does not work in an office.
shef mol okupa. = the boss is very busy.

lesson paying
+ paga | to pay | verb
shef zil paga. = the boss pays money.
mi rabo i zil ha. = I work and I have money.
tu bilet paga deba. = you must pay for the ticket.
kwi paga? = who pays?
aba yamë paga. = father pays for the food.

lesson the calendar
+ kalendar | calendar | noun
+ data | date | noun
kalendar un ofis es. = the calendar is in the office.
vedey data kwo? = what is the date today?
nasdey da mi un kalendar es. = my birthday is on the calendar.
mi kalendar nov kopata. = I bought a new calendar.
ve data bon. = this date is good.

lesson on time
bus tarda, mi tarda. = the bus is late, I am late.
tu un or nir ven deba. = you must come at nine o'clock.
mi un or okh venta. = I came at eight o'clock.
mi preko esta. = I was early.
mi tarda ne esva. = I will not be late.
