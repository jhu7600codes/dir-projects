section 5 | speaking freely | society, science, art and your own story

module 13 | community

lesson my town
new: limangvar, vlëmen, dali, kechanu, nirun
mi un vlëmen pik viva. = I live in a small town.
dali da min lëbë. = our community is friendly.
baba un limangvar viva. = grandmother lives in a village.
ve kechanu bonesh da vlëmen. = this is the best place in town.
boteka nirun pik i bela. = the local shop is small and beautiful.

lesson helping out
new: teta, beni, kavobu, nodusa, zanlë
mi un kavobu beni. = I volunteer at a charity.
tetan multa ayda. = many volunteers help.
min kamisan vet nodusata. = we donated old shirts.
zanlë da tu vala. = your donation is important.
mi ayda goda, kaus felatal do. = I like to help because it gives happiness.

lesson meetings
new: dunur, khashi, keyer, fili, zache
vedey dunur da dali es. = today there is a community meeting.
keyer dunur khashita. = the leader organized the meeting.
tot mulal da sa thelata. = everyone said their opinion.
fili lon esta. = the debate was long.
un fin, min zacheta. = in the end, we agreed.

lesson town rules
new: bonezam, merfe, pesklëga, gim, gabël, sepe
bonezam vala. = the law is important.
gim ik sepe ne merfe. = the police do not allow parking here.
parka vufan merfe. = the park allows dogs.
skol ronishan pesklëga. = the school forbids phones.
gabël human ayda. = the police officer helps people.

lesson voting
new: nëki, riri, dihi, mafalak, duse, wusa
posdey nëki esva. = tomorrow there will be an election.
aba i ama riri. = father and mother vote.
human mafalak nov dihita. = the people elected a new president.
duse reglan nov fasa. = the government makes new rules.
kwan mi wusava, mi ririva. = when I grow up, I will vote.

lesson rights and duties
new: romzom, thunatvush, leni, nabranuk, liluk
human tot romzom ha. = all people have rights.
human tot leni. = all people are equal.
nuten thunatvushan ha. = a citizen has duties.
nabranuk i liluk vala. = freedom and justice are important.
min romzoman da sinang shelmi deba. = we must respect the rights of others.

lesson protests
new: iket, pota, alfi, givri, pekro, bena
human alfi bonezam nov pota. = people protest against the new law.
iket un plaza esta. = the protest was in the square.
ve bonezam pekro. = this law is unfair.
mi iket givri. = I support the protest.
iket bena esta. = the protest was peaceful.

lesson guests and strangers
new: krazan, lattang, kiblar
krazan un vilë mi kwetata. = a stranger in the street asked me.
min vedey lattangan ha. = we have guests today.
kiblar bon lattangan devo. = a good host welcomes guests.
vesinan kun yama. = the neighbors eat together.
krazan vren nov esta. = the stranger was a new friend.

lesson jobs in town
new: rekping, nelishu, taka, bimni, kila, tukhu, tudi
rekping human salva. = the firefighter saves people.
nelishu un taka rabo. = the nurse works in the hospital.
bimni leguman kila. = the farmer plants vegetables.
tukhu tudi nov waba. = the builder builds a new building.
rab tot vala. = every job is important.

lesson a better town
sef min kun rabo, dali bonor esva. = if we work together, the community will be better.
mi teta es vola. = I want to be a volunteer.
vesinan ayda! = help the neighbors!
ming min lute, min leni. = although we are different, we are equal.
dali da min fela esva. = our community will be happy.

module 13.5 | big questions

lesson rich and poor
new: dapa, shali, mobë, vëlvro
un dapa, human teli i naske es. = in society, there are rich and poor people.
shali multa un khanan da teli es. = a lot of power is in the hands of the rich.
mi penka ke ve pekro. = I think that this is unfair.
sef human tot vëlvrota, dapa bonor esve. = if everyone shared, society would be better.
mobë vrenan, tot leni. = among friends, everyone is equal.

lesson crime and justice
new: boku, neza, rinush, dlukasi, zim, pasho
boku mal. = crime is bad.
dar zil nezata. = a man stole money.
gim sa trovata. = the police found him.
dlukasi i zim un pasho rabo. = the judge and the lawyer work in court.
sa ra rinush golta. = he went to prison.

lesson right and wrong
new: goru, ebel
ve regla ekwa ka? = is this rule fair?
mi penka ke sa obru. = I think that it is wrong.
ebel da tu kwo? = what is your reason?
mulal da tu goru. = your opinion is correct.
tot ebel ha. = everyone has a reason.

lesson speeches
new: fepamos, lusela, sichë, kakri
keyer fepamos lon fasata. = the leader gave a long speech.
sa human lusela shupita. = she tried to convince the people.
mi mulal da mi sichë vola. = I want to express my opinion.
min un skol kakri. = we debate at school.
fepamos da sa mi luselata. = her speech convinced me.

lesson what happened?
new: arar, gaksi, petos, zosepo, sosvan, vra
kwo gaksita? = what happened?
hafi arar lebis nëki skrita. = a journalist wrote an article about the election.
petosan vedey zosepova. = the results will appear today.
sosvan gran gaksita. = something big happened.
vra lebis sa thela. = everybody talks about it.

lesson helping the world
new: wigotu, ramigo
min ramigo ra mundo. = we are responsible for the world.
kavobu wigotu human naske ayda. = a global charity helps poor people.
mi zil pik nodusata. = I donated a little money.
shin pik multamo ayda pot. = small things can help a lot.
tot sosvan fasa pot. = everyone can do something.

lesson old and new
new: sasru, fozas, gahu
baba lolivon goda. = grandmother likes traditions.
kitin shin sasru goda. = children like modern things.
dapa sempo muda. = society always changes.
fozas ne mal. = change is not bad.
ve gahu un nasho da min. = this is normal in our country.

lesson even if
new: rastëtra
tip: rastëtra (even if) starts its own part, like sef.
rastëtra tu ne kreda, sa ver. = even if you do not believe it, it is true.
rastëtra pluva, mi golva. = even if it rains, I will go.
rastëtra sa naske, sa rugetë. = even if he is poor, he is generous.
rastëtra ve khar, min nidiva. = even if this is hard, we will succeed.
rastëtra tot ridava, mi lirava. = even if everyone laughs, I will sing.

lesson as long as
new: vro, loli
tip: vro (as long as) starts its own part too.
vro min kun es, min forë. = as long as we are together, we are strong.
vro tu trena, tu loli. = as long as you practice, you improve.
vro sol shina, kitin teshi luda. = as long as the sun shines, the children play outside.
vro tu honesta, mi tu fida. = as long as you are honest, I trust you.
vro mi viva, mi tu livava. = as long as I live, I will love you.

lesson your opinion
mi penka ke human tot leni. = I think that all people are equal.
ming min lute, min shelmi deba. = although we are different, we must respect.
mulal da tu vala. = your opinion is important.
kiro, mulal da tu sichë! = please express your opinion!
vro min thela, min lerna. = as long as we talk, we learn.

module 14 | science and space

lesson science class
new: dozim, gino, vute, bowo, elmisha
mi dozim goda. = I like science.
gino tote kisni. = biology studies life.
vute un elmisha es. = chemistry is in the laboratory.
bowo khar, shom tamchin. = physics is hard but interesting.
min un elmisha rabo. = we work in the laboratory.

lesson experiments
new: lëshi, tulu, blutri, tosëga, pangkhe
min lëshi fasa. = we do an experiment.
mi nami blutri. = I observe the water.
petos tamchin esta. = the result was interesting.
pangkhe ideya da sa tosëgata. = the scientist proved her idea.
sef tu tulu, tu lerna. = if you experiment, you learn.

lesson tiny things
new: gara, tofe, liyem, milur, vapu
vuvo garan ha. = everything has atoms.
vapu tofen multa ha. = the body has many cells.
mi tofen kel liyem blutri. = I observe cells with a microscope.
gara mol milur. = an atom is very tiny.
liyem shin milur poshna. = a microscope shows tiny things.

lesson space
new: tunthan, krili, prit, lëlnë, atgok, vabok
tunthan vabok. = the universe is endless.
tera un krili gran es. = the earth is in a big galaxy.
mi stelan kel prit blutri. = I observe stars with a telescope.
lëlnë un vin esta! = there was a comet in the sky!
atgok min sur tera chati. = gravity holds us on the earth.

lesson astronauts
new: rasi, zipo, ungnem, latha
rasi ra kosmo ala. = the astronaut flies to space.
zipo mol rap. = the rocket is very fast.
ungnem yordo esta. = the moon landing was long ago.
human sosvan gran lathata. = people achieved something big.
mi rasi es vola! = I want to be an astronaut!

lesson dinosaurs
new: midisin, rorang, gibi
midisinan yordo vivata. = dinosaurs lived long ago.
pangkhe rorang nov gibita. = a scientist discovered a new fossil.
ve midisin granor dë elefan esta. = this dinosaur was bigger than an elephant.
nun midisinan ne es. = now there are no dinosaurs.
mi rorangan un muzë vidta. = I saw fossils in the museum.

lesson our restless planet
new: rukpavo, bartival, lilu, ritëm, lonat, fotho
rukpavo lonat. = the volcano is dangerous.
predey bartival esta. = yesterday there was an earthquake.
apre fotho multa, lilu esta. = after a lot of rain, there was a flood.
un somër, ritëm es. = in summer, there is a drought.
human kun ayda. = people help together.

lesson inventions
new: yatomso, fodas, vihubrol, kudu, gozar
fodas vihubrol nov yatomsota. = the inventor invented a new machine.
kudu mol kitshuk. = electricity is very useful.
vihubrol gozar vashingvi. = the machine needs energy.
mi sosvan nov yatomso vola. = I want to invent something new.
kwi kompu yatomsota? = who invented the computer?

lesson why?
new: pëngmo
mi masoplo kwa vin blu. = I wonder why the sky is blue.
pangkhen pëngmo. = scientists research.
dozim ebelan poshna. = science shows the reasons.
mi tunthan rava vola. = I want to understand the universe.
kwesto bon sempo vala. = a good question is always important.

lesson the science fair
new: kure, bosho, zarka
vedey sopu da dozim es. = today there is a science festival.
mi shemde pik kureta. = I designed a small robot.
shemde da mi bosho vinata! = my robot won a prize!
mestro mol zarka esta. = the teacher was very proud.
posdey mi lëshi nov fasava. = tomorrow I will do a new experiment.

module 14.5 | the environment

lesson climate
new: kiteka, thechu, domu, kothi
kiteka muda. = the climate is changing.
thechu domu. = the temperature increases.
somëran kalor esva. = summers will be hotter.
kothi vala. = nature is important.
min kothi garda deba. = we must protect nature.

lesson pollution
new: loyu, trarba, morlan, tatak, urliper
loyu ra kothi mal. = pollution is bad for nature.
oton morlan multa do. = cars give a lot of smoke.
tanë gran ruma trarba. = a big company pollutes the river.
tatak multa un tate es. = there is a lot of plastic in the ocean.
min la urliper mengpa deba. = we must use less gas.

lesson recycling
new: efa, binu, chibumi, pininom
mi pepan binu. = I recycle bottles.
resto ne chibumi, binu! = do not throw away trash, recycle!
pininom yeran multa resta. = plastic stays for many years.
efa kitshuk. = recycling is useful.
un skol, min papir i pininom binu. = at school, we recycle paper and plastic.

lesson clean energy
new: deteng, kharo
deteng gozar dos sol fasa. = a solar panel makes energy from the sun.
kharo gozar dos khal fasa. = a wind turbine makes energy from the wind.
kar da min deteng ha. = our house has a solar panel.
gozar puro vala. = clean energy is important.
avenë da gozar puro bon. = the future of clean energy is good.

lesson animals in danger
new: krikaresh, sashne, ode, eshshi, notbi
krikareshan multa sashne. = many species disappear.
eshshin un tate ode deba. = whales must survive in the ocean.
lada sashne, kaus human talan notbi. = the jungle disappears because people cut trees.
min letrukan garda deba. = we must protect animals.
sef min ne ayda, krikareshan sashneva. = if we do not help, species will disappear.

lesson wasting less
new: kudu, kishum
nami ne lesi! = do not waste water!
kwan tu rum redu, lampan utho! = when you leave the room, turn off the lamps!
mi yamë ne lesi. = I do not waste food.
la kopa, gras binu. = buy less, recycle more.
kudu kishummo mengpa! = use electricity wisely!

lesson trees
new: males, bili
min talan kila. = we plant trees.
talan khal puro do. = trees give clean air.
males dos talan ven. = wood comes from trees.
silva ra kothi vala. = the forest is important for nature.
bili verdë un gardo es. = there is green grass in the garden.

lesson what I can do
mi kel biko ra skol gol pot. = I can go to school by bike.
mi pininom la mengpa pot. = I can use less plastic.
mi talan kila pot. = I can plant trees.
mi vrenan lebis kothi dosha pot. = I can teach friends about nature.
tot sosvan fasa pot. = everyone can do something.

lesson hope for the earth
new: hiru, zapu
mi hiru ha. = I have hope.
mi zapu. = I am hopeful.
sef min muda, tera odeva. = if we change, the earth will survive.
avenë un khanan da min es. = the future is in our hands.
min kun mundo garda pot. = together we can protect the world.

lesson earth day
vedey dey da tera es. = today is earth day.
min un parka resto limpata. = we cleaned trash in the park.
min dek tal kilata. = we planted ten trees.
tot fela i zarka esta. = everyone was happy and proud.
tera da min bela. = our earth is beautiful.

module 15 | art and expression

lesson painting
new: ashok, nodlëmu, vetho, dadarfëm, takmarvak, ukhis
ashok sur nodlëmu pinta. = the painter paints on a canvas.
vethon multa ra mi do! = give me many colors!
dadarfëm da mi ukhis. = my brush is thin.
kuadro takmarvak bela. = the colorful picture is beautiful.
vetho favo da tu kwo? = what is your favorite color?

lesson galleries
new: bosung, ratal, oma, mumune, bafel
min ra bosung golta. = we went to the gallery.
ratal gran un plaza es. = a big statue is in the square.
bafel oma nov mumuneta. = the artist sculpted a new sculpture.
kuadron un bosung thunpo. = the pictures in the gallery are ancient.
mi bafel es vola. = I want to be an artist.

lesson creativity
new: kotpim, womis, uda, lupe, rama
kitin mol kotpim. = children are very creative.
womis da tu gran! = your imagination is big!
mi uda dos kothi trova. = I find inspiration in nature.
lupe ke tu ala pot! = imagine that you can fly!
mi sosvan bela rama vola. = I want to create something beautiful.

lesson instruments

kheguki da ve lir bela. = the melody of this song is beautiful.

lesson writing songs
new: putamet, newa, tire, roge
mi lir nov tire. = I compose a new song.

ve thelan newa ha. = these words have a rhyme.

mi lir da mi ra tu lirava. = I will sing my song for you.

lesson the theater

biflikan sur vlunni zozo. = the actors perform on the stage.

mi biflik es vola. = I want to be an actor.

lesson poetry
new: naka, emkat, esal, zimar, mosho
esal emkat bela skrita. = the poet wrote a beautiful poem.
naka mosho ha. = poetry has beauty.
mi emkat lebis pelag skrita. = I wrote a poem about the sea.
zimar da nasho da min vet. = the literature of our country is old.

lesson talent and practice
new: pisshe, devi, yuku, batdung, fupang, li

devi yuku vashingvi. = skill needs practice.
batdung vala. = patience is important.
sef tu dey tot trena, tu loli. = if you practice every day, you improve.

lesson feelings in art
new: decha, tomom, banung, zerda, venë

muzik dechan sichë. = music expresses emotions.
ve lir mol tomom. = this song is very emotional.
banung i zerda un arta es. = there is joy and sadness in art.
kwan mi ve lir ota, mi venë. = when I hear this song, I cry.

lesson the art show
vedey shoa da arta es. = today there is an art show.
kuadron da mi un bosung es! = my pictures are in the gallery!
human multa venta. = many people came.
tot kuadron da mi godata. = everyone liked my pictures.
mi mol zarka. = I am very proud.

module 15.5 | your own story

lesson childhood
new: teshdë, zupro, lëdak
mi un urba pik zuprota. = I was born in a small city.
teshdë da mi fela esta. = my childhood was happy.
kwan mi lëdak esta, mi multamo ludata. = when I was young, I played a lot.
mi un ferma wusata. = I grew up on a farm.
mi teshdë da mi rekorda. = I remember my childhood.

lesson friendship
new: sispikyi, kozu, nirim, godi, kola, lash
sispikyi valar dë zil. = friendship is more important than money.
nirim un sispikyi rekame. = trust is necessary in a friendship.
vren da mi kolata i mi sa godita. = my friend apologized and I forgave him.
kozu da min forë. = our relationship is strong.
sispikyi lash resta. = friendship stays forever.

lesson courage
new: dukel, vishdreruk, museng, otbek
dukel vala. = courage is important.
mi otbek, shom mi gol. = I am nervous, but I go.
hum vishdreruk museng ha, shom sa gol. = a brave person has fear, but she goes.
ming mi timë esta, mi nidita. = although I was scared, I succeeded.
museng ne vabok. = fear is not endless.

lesson mistakes
new: vënëshim, febu, var, kopayo, koklo, puchuso
vënëshim ne mal. = a mistake is not bad.
mi kopayota, shom mi koklota. = I failed, but I continued.
dos febu, min lerna. = from failure, we learn.
var batdung vashingvi. = success needs patience.
ne puchuso! = do not stop!

lesson meaning
new: kranu, katat, tase, maru
katat da tote kwo? = what is the meaning of life?
tot kranu kasha ha. = everyone has their own purpose.
mi maru ke mi ayda pot. = I hope that I can help.
tase da mi forë. = my belief is strong.
tote dara. = life is a gift.

lesson your future
new: salong, fipi, klutpru
salong mi sonyo da mi lathava. = someday I will achieve my dream.
fipi! = dream big!
mi klutpru ke tot fela. = I wish that everyone is happy.
avenë da tu bela esva. = your future will be beautiful.
tu sosvan gran latha pot. = you can achieve something big.

lesson thank you
new: danzizi, inana
mi ra folka da mi ika. = I am grateful to my family.
mi mestro da mi vichi. = I thank my teacher.
danzizi pik mundo muda pot. = a small kindness can change the world.
inana i lamor vala. = honesty and respect are important.
shevan ra tot! = thanks to everyone!

lesson your story
new: lidro, woshla, popa, sarwu
tot sago ha. = everyone has a story.
sago da tu ra mi sarwu! = tell me your story!
mi tote da mi lidro. = I describe my life.
kiro, ve woshla. = please explain this.
sa popata ke sago da sa lon. = she said that her story is long.

lesson everything together
sef mi ripë kiti esta, mi gras ludave. = if I were a child again, I would play more.
ming tote khar, tote bela. = although life is hard, life is beautiful.
nirfung tu viva, lerna! = while you live, learn!
vro tu sonya, tu nidi pot. = as long as you dream, you can succeed.
rastëtra tu kopayo, koklo! = even if you fail, continue!

lesson the last lesson
new: lisra
tu vendëri lisra kenë. = you already know Venderian.
nun tu thela, skri i rava pot. = now you can speak, write and understand.
sago da tu un vendëri skri! = write your story in Venderian!
vendëri sempo un kor da tu esva. = Venderian will always be in your heart.
dano, vren. shevan! = goodbye, friend. thank you!
