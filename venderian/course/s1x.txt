# section 1, lessons 6-10 of every module (merged into s1.txt's modules by make_course.py)

section 1 | first steps | family, food and home

module 1 | family!

lesson how old are you?
new: kwo, dek, yer
+ edë | age | noun
tip: to say how old you are, have years: "mi dek yer ha", I have ten years.
edë da tu kwo? = what is your age?
mi dek yer ha. = I am ten years old.
bro da mi dor yer ha. = my brother is two years old.
tu dek yer ha ka? = are you ten years old?
sesa da ana ek yer ha. = Ana's sister is one year old.

lesson names
new: ishe, kwi
+ lela | Lela (a name) | name
ishe da tu kwo? = what is your name?
ishe da mi tomo. = my name is Tomo.
kwi sa? = who is he?
sa bro da mi. = he is my brother.
ishe da sesa da mi lela. = my sister's name is Lela.

lesson aunts and uncles
+ tiya | aunt | noun
+ tiyo | uncle | noun
+ kuzo | cousin | noun
tiya da mi tis kiti ha. = my aunt has three children.
tiyo da tu gran ka? = is your uncle big?
mi dor kuzo ha. = I have two cousins.
tiya i tiyo vet. = the aunt and uncle are old.
kuzo da ana tomo. = Ana's cousin is Tomo.

lesson the baby
new: nov, nema, liva
+ bebë | baby | noun
folka da mi bebë nov ha. = my family has a new baby.
bebë pik. = the baby is small.
bebë nema. = the baby sleeps.
bebë da sesa da mi nema ka? = is my sister's baby sleeping?
baba bebë liva. = grandmother loves the baby.

lesson a family picture
new: ik, lë
+ kuadro | picture | noun
ve kuadro da folka da mi. = this is a picture of my family.
ik ama i aba. = here are mother and father.
lë baba i dedo. = there are grandmother and grandfather.
kwi ve? = who is this?
ve tiyo da mi. = this is my uncle.

module 1.5 | family traditions

lesson birthday
new: vedey, fasa, kwan, fela
+ nasdey | birthday | noun
+ torta | cake | noun
vedey nasdey da mi! = today is my birthday!
ama torta fasa. = mother makes a cake.
tot dara ra mi do. = everyone gives a gift to me.
mi mol fela. = I am very happy.
nasdey da tu kwan? = when is your birthday?

lesson songs at home
new: lira, lir, kenë, bela
baba lir vet lira. = grandmother sings an old song.
kitin kun lira. = the children sing together.
dedo liran multa kenë. = grandfather knows many songs.
tu ve lir kenë ka? = do you know this song?
ve lir bela. = this song is beautiful.

lesson grandmother's food
new: skri
+ resita | recipe | noun
baba yamë bon fasa. = grandmother makes good food.
resita da baba vet. = grandmother's recipe is old.
min yamë da baba liva. = we love grandmother's food.
resita ra mi do! = give the recipe to me!
ama resita skri. = mother writes the recipe.

lesson visiting
new: ven, es
+ vizita | to visit | verb
+ saven | often | adverb
min baba vizita. = we visit grandmother.
tiyo saven ven. = the uncle comes often.
tu dedo saven vizita ka? = do you visit grandfather often?
kuzon da mi ik ven. = my cousins come here.
folka tot kun es. = the whole family is together.

lesson thank you, family
new: shevan, ayda
shevan, ama! = thank you, mother!
aba mi ayda. = father helps me.
mi ama ayda. = I help mother.
folka da mi mi liva. = my family loves me.
shevan, folka! = thank you, family!

module 2 | food

lesson vegetables
+ legum | vegetable | noun
+ karot | carrot | noun
+ patat | potato | noun
+ tomat | tomato | noun
leguman bon. = vegetables are good.
karot gel. = the carrot is yellow.
mi patatan goda. = I like potatoes.
tomat rud i sava. = the tomato is red and sweet.
kiti leguman ne goda. = the child does not like vegetables.

lesson meat and fish

+ karnë | meat | noun

mi glob goda. = I like fish.
ama glob kuka. = mother cooks fish.

tu karnë o glob vola? = do you want meat or fish?

lesson drinks
new: kiro
+ suko | juice | noun
+ glasë | glass | noun
mi suko vola. = I want juice.

suko sava. = the juice is sweet.
kafa kal, suko frid. = the coffee is hot, the juice is cold.
tu kwo sipa? = what do you drink?

lesson the restaurant

lesson sweet things

+ kandi | candy | noun

tu choko vola ka? = do you want chocolate?
ama choko ne yama. = mother does not eat chocolate.

module 2.5 | cooking and meals

lesson lunch
new: nun
+ medey | lunch | noun
un medey, mi supa yama. = at lunch, I eat soup.
medey kwan? = when is lunch?
min medey kun yama. = we eat lunch together.
ve medey mol bon. = this lunch is very good.
nun mi medey vola. = now I want lunch.

lesson the shopping list
new: lon
+ lista | list | noun
mi lista skri. = I write a list.
lista lon. = the list is long.
aplon, bren i mikh un lista es. = apples, bread and milk are on the list.
min un bazar kopa. = we buy at the market.
lista da ama kwe es? = where is mother's list?

lesson setting the table
+ kup | cup | noun
+ kulyë | spoon | noun
+ forko | fork | noun

kulyë i forko ra mi do. = give me a spoon and a fork.
forko kwe es? = where is the fork?
nash sur plato es. = the knife is on the plate.
tabla bela. = the table is beautiful.

lesson cleaning up
+ limpa | to clean | verb
mi platon limpa. = I clean the plates.
aba kukin limpa. = father cleans the kitchen.
kitin ayda. = the children help.
nami kal bon. = hot water is good.
tot kun limpa. = everyone cleans together.

lesson favorite food
+ favo | favorite | adjective
yamë favo da tu kwo? = what is your favorite food?
yamë favo da mi supa. = my favorite food is soup.
frut favo da sa banan. = her favorite fruit is the banana.
ama torta favo da mi fasa. = mother makes my favorite cake.
tu choko goda ka? = do you like chocolate?

module 3 | home

lesson the bathroom
+ twalet | toilet | noun
+ spegë | mirror | noun
+ savon | soap | noun
twalet kwe es? = where is the toilet?
spegë gran. = the mirror is big.
savon nek spegë es. = the soap is next to the mirror.
mi spegë un rum da mi ha. = I have a mirror in my room.
savon alba. = the soap is white.

lesson upstairs and downstairs
new: gol
+ supra | upstairs | adverb
+ infra | downstairs | adverb
+ skala | stairs | noun
rum da mi supra es. = my room is upstairs.
kukin infra es. = the kitchen is downstairs.
skala gran. = the stairs are big.
vuf supra ne gol. = the dog does not go upstairs.
mish infra nema. = the cat sleeps downstairs.

lesson the garden
new: gala, luda
+ gardo | garden | noun
kar da mi gardo ha. = my house has a garden.
galan multa un gardo es. = there are many flowers in the garden.
tal gran un gardo es. = a big tree is in the garden.
vuf un gardo luda. = the dog plays in the garden.
gardo bela. = the garden is beautiful.

lesson cleaning the house
+ polvo | dust | noun
vedey min kar limpa. = today we clean the house.
polvo multa sur tabla es. = there is a lot of dust on the table.
aba vindë limpa. = father cleans the window.
mi rum da mi limpa. = I clean my room.
kar nun bela. = now the house is beautiful.

lesson neighbors
+ vesin | neighbor | noun
vesin da mi vet. = my neighbor is old.
vesin vuf gran ha. = the neighbor has a big dog.
vesinan bon. = the neighbors are good.
vesin un kar alba viva. = the neighbor lives in a white house.
tu vesin kenë ka? = do you know the neighbor?

module 3.5 | daily routine

lesson clothes
+ kamis | shirt | noun
+ pantal | pants | noun
+ shu | shoe | noun
mi kamis blu ha. = I have a blue shirt.
pantal da tu mog. = your pants are black.
shun da mi kwe es? = where are my shoes?
kiti shun nov ha. = the child has new shoes.
ve kamis bela. = this shirt is beautiful.

lesson getting dressed
+ vesta | to put on (clothes) | verb
mi kamis vesta. = I put on a shirt.
un matin, kiti shun vesta. = in the morning, the child puts on shoes.
kamis rud vesta! = put on the red shirt!
aba kamis alba vesta. = father puts on a white shirt.
tu kwo vesta? = what do you put on?

lesson hurry up!
+ tarda | late | adjective
+ preko | early | adjective
mi tarda! = I am late!
tu tarda ka? = are you late?
nay, mi preko. = no, I am early.
gol! gol! = go! go!
ama preko leva. = mother gets up early.

lesson bedtime
+ noksa | pajamas | noun
un noch, mi noksa vesta. = at night, I put on pajamas.
ama sago thela. = mother tells a story.
lampa klu! = turn off the lamp!
kiti nema vola. = the child wants to sleep.
noch bon! = good night!

lesson the weekend
+ fendey | weekend | noun
un fendey, mi skol ne gol. = on the weekend, I do not go to school.
un fendey, min un gardo luda. = on the weekend, we play in the garden.
aba un fendey nema. = father sleeps on the weekend.
fendey kwan? = when is the weekend?
mi fendey liva. = I love the weekend.
