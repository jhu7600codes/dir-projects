# section 3, lessons 6-10 of every module

section 3 | talking for real | feelings, stories, hobbies and big ideas

module 7 | feelings and health

lesson tired and bored
+ fatiga | tired | adjective
+ tedo | bored | adjective
mi fatiga, kaus mi multamo rabota. = I am tired because I worked a lot.
kiti tedo. = the child is bored.
sef tu fatiga, nema! = if you are tired, sleep!
tu tedo ka? = are you bored?
min tedo ne esta. = we were not bored.

lesson love
+ kisa | to kiss | verb
+ abraza | to hug | verb
ama kiti abraza. = mother hugs the child.
mi tu liva. = I love you.
aba ama kisata. = father kissed mother.
kor da mi fela. = my heart is happy.
sef tu mi liva, mi fela. = if you love me, I am happy.

lesson worries
+ kwida | to worry | verb
ama multamo kwida. = mother worries a lot.
ne kwida! = do not worry!
mi kwida, kaus prova khar. = I worry because the test is hard.
tu kwa kwida? = why do you worry?
aba ne kwida. = father does not worry.

lesson surprises
+ surpriza | surprise | noun
mi surpriza ra tu ha. = I have a surprise for you.
surpriza bela esta! = the surprise was beautiful!
baba surpriza ne kenëta. = grandmother did not know about the surprise.
sef tu surpriza goda, mi fela. = if you like the surprise, I am happy.
surpriza kwe es? = where is the surprise?

lesson staying healthy
+ aktiva | active | adjective
leguman yama i sana es! = eat vegetables and be healthy!
sef tu aktiva, tu sana. = if you are active, you are healthy.
aba dey tot pasa. = father walks every day.
nami multa sipa! = drink a lot of water!
mi sana senta. = I feel healthy.

module 7.5 | friends and stories

lesson fairy tales
+ feya | fairy | noun
+ magik | magic | noun
feya magik ha. = the fairy has magic.
sago da feya mol bela. = the story of the fairy is very beautiful.
feya ki un silva viva pik. = the fairy who lives in the forest is small.
magik ver ka? = is magic real?
kitin feyan goda. = the children like fairies.

lesson heroes
+ heroë | hero | noun
+ salva | to save | verb
heroë kiti salvata. = the hero saved the child.
heroë ki kiti salvata forë. = the hero who saved the child is strong.
mi heroë es vola. = I want to be a hero.
heroë da mi aba. = my hero is my father.
kwi tu salvata? = who saved you?

lesson secrets
+ sekret | secret | noun
mi sekret ha. = I have a secret.
mi sekret ra tu thela ne pot. = I cannot tell you the secret.
vren bonesh da mi sekretan da mi kenë. = my best friend knows my secrets.
sekret ki tu thelata ver ka? = is the secret that you told true?
ve sekret da min. = this is our secret.

lesson a letter
+ letra | letter | noun
mi letra ra vren skrita. = I wrote a letter to my friend.
letra lon esta. = the letter was long.
vren letra ripë mandata. = my friend sent a letter again.
letra ki tu mandata mol bela. = the letter that you sent is very beautiful.
letra kwan venva? = when will the letter come?

lesson jokes
+ shaka | joke | noun
aba shakan thela. = father tells jokes.
ve shaka mol bon! = this joke is very good!
tot ridata. = everyone laughed.
shaka ki tu thelata ne bon. = the joke that you told is not good.
mi shaka nov kenë. = I know a new joke.

module 8 | hobbies and music

lesson reading
+ novela | novel | noun
+ autor | author | noun
mi novelan goda. = I like novels.
autor da ve novela vet. = the author of this novel is old.
mi dey tot ridë. = I read every day.
novela ki mi ridë lon. = the novel that I read is long.
tu autor favo ha ka? = do you have a favorite author?

lesson movies
+ filmo | movie | noun
+ kino | cinema | noun
min ra kino golta. = we went to the cinema.
filmo mol bon esta. = the movie was very good.
mi filmon goda. = I like movies.
filmo ki min vidta lon esta. = the movie that we saw was long.
filmo favo da tu kwo? = what is your favorite movie?

lesson cooking for fun
mi kuka goda. = I like to cook.
aba kukari bon. = father is a good cook.
mi torta nov fasata. = I made a new cake.
sef tu kuka, mi yamava. = if you cook, I will eat.
ve resita lekhor dë vo. = this recipe is easier than that one.

lesson collecting
new: pira
+ kolekta | to collect | verb
+ stampo | stamp | noun
dedo stampon kolekta. = grandfather collects stamps.
mi piran kolekta. = I collect stones.
stampon da dedo vet. = grandfather's stamps are old.
tu kwo kolekta? = what do you collect?
mi pira bela trovata. = I found a beautiful stone.

lesson photos
+ kamera | camera | noun
mi kamera nov ha. = I have a new camera.
mi foton da pitan fasa. = I take photos of birds.
ve foto belash. = this photo is the most beautiful.
kamera da tu karo ka? = is your camera expensive?
sef sol shina, foton bonor. = if the sun shines, the photos are better.

module 8.5 | games and sport

lesson swimming
+ nada | to swim | verb
+ piskina | pool | noun
mi nada pot. = I can swim.
min un piskina nadata. = we swam in the pool.
nami un piskina frid. = the water in the pool is cold.
tu rapmo nada ka? = do you swim fast?
kiti nada lerna. = the child is learning to swim.

lesson bikes
+ biko | bike | noun
mi biko rud ha. = I have a red bike.
min kel biko ra parka golta. = we went to the park by bike.
biko da tu nov ka? = is your bike new?
aba biko kondu ne pot. = father cannot ride a bike.
biko rapor dë oto un urba. = a bike is faster than a car in the city.

lesson the race
+ korso | race | noun
vedey korso gran es. = today there is a big race.
mi un korso kurata. = I ran in a race.
kwi korso vinata? = who won the race?
sesa da mi rapesh esta. = my sister was the fastest.
sef tu trena, tu korso vinava. = if you train, you will win the race.

lesson video games
+ video | video | noun
mi ludon da video goda. = I like video games.
aba ludon da video ne goda. = father does not like video games.
ve ludo nov i rap. = this game is new and fast.
mi kel vren un vechë luda. = I play with a friend in the evening.
ludo ki tu kopata bon. = the game that you bought is good.

lesson a good team
ekipa bon kun trena. = a good team trains together.
mi un ekipa da skol es. = I am on the school team.
ludarin da ekipa da min vrenan. = the players of our team are friends.
kwan min perda, min ne angra. = when we lose, we are not angry.
ekipa ki kun rabo vina. = a team that works together wins.

module 9 | ideas and opinions

lesson rules
reglan vala. = rules are important.
min reglan da skol kenë deba. = we must know the school rules.
ve regla ne ekwa. = this rule is not fair.
mi penka ke reglan multa mal. = I think that many rules are bad.
tu kel mi konsa ka? = do you agree with me?

lesson the internet
new: shi
+ reto | internet | noun
mi un reto multamo lerna. = I learn a lot on the internet.
shi tot un reto ne ver. = not everything on the internet is true.
reto rap. = the internet is fast.
ve kompu reto ne ha. = this computer does not have internet.
sef tu reto ne ha, ridë! = if you do not have internet, read!

lesson money and happiness
zil felatal ne do. = money does not give happiness.
mi penka ke zil vala. = I think that money is important.
vrenan valar dë zil. = friends are more important than money.
hum ki zil multa ha ne fela. = the person who has a lot of money is not happy.
mi kel tu ne konsa. = I do not agree with you.

lesson protecting the earth
min tera garda deba. = we must protect the earth.
sef min tera ne garda, avenë mal esva. = if we do not protect the earth, the future will be bad.
avenë da kitin vala. = the future of the children is important.
mi penka ke min ayda pot. = I think that we can help.
min kun rabo deba. = we must work together.

lesson a good person
+ honesta | honest | adjective
+ dulsë | kind | adjective
hum bon honesta. = a good person is honest.
dulsëtal vala. = kindness is important.
ama da mi mol dulsë. = my mother is very kind.
sef tu honesta, human tu fida. = if you are honest, people trust you.
mi penka ke tu hum bon. = I think that you are a good person.

module 9.5 | big conversations

lesson the news
+ nova | news | noun
nova vedey bon. = the news today is good.
tu nova otata ka? = did you hear the news?
mi nova un reto ridë. = I read the news on the internet.
nova ki sa thelata ver ka? = is the news that she told true?
mi penka ke nova fal. = I think that the news is false.

lesson making plans
+ invita | to invite | verb
mi tu ra festa invita. = I invite you to the party.
tu dey pami libër ka? = are you free on Friday?
min kun ra kino golva. = we will go to the cinema together.
sef pluva, min un kar restava i filmo vidva. = if it rains, we will stay at home and watch a movie.
plan bon! = good plan!

lesson saying sorry
+ pardon | sorry | interjection
pardon! = sorry!
pardon, mi tarda. = sorry, I am late.
pardon, mi ishe da tu oblidata. = sorry, I forgot your name.
ne kwida! = do not worry!
mi tarda ne esva. = I will not be late.

lesson advice
+ konsel | advice | noun
mi konsel ra tu ha. = I have advice for you.
konsel da mi, dey tot lerna. = my advice, learn every day.
sef tu fatiga, nema deba. = if you are tired, you must sleep.
konsel da baba sempo bon. = grandmother's advice is always good.
tu konsel ra mi do pot ka? = can you give me advice?

lesson you made it
new: dosha
mi mol fela, kaus tu vendëri lernata. = I am very happy because you learned Venderian.
tu heroë da vendëri! = you are a Venderian hero!
tu vendëri ra vrenan dosha pot. = you can teach Venderian to friends.
sef tu thela, mi ota. = if you speak, I listen.
dano i shevan! = goodbye and thank you!
