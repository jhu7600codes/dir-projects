section 1

module 1 | family!

story the new friend
ana: vela! mi ana. tu tomo ka? = hello! I am Ana. are you Tomo?
tomo: ya, mi tomo. = yes, I am Tomo.
ana: tu bro ha ka? = do you have a brother?
tomo: nay. mi dor sesa ha. = no. I have two sisters.
? how many sisters does Tomo have? | two* | one | three
ana: folka da mi gran. baba i dedo vet. = my family is big. grandmother and grandfather are old.
tomo: folka da mi pik. = my family is small.
? whose family is big? | Ana's* | Tomo's | nobody's
ana: dano, tomo! = goodbye, Tomo!

challenge story the family picture
lela: ve kuadro da folka da mi. = this is a picture of my family.
tomo: kwi sa? = who is he?
lela: sa kuzo da mi. sa dek yer ha. = he is my cousin. he is ten years old.
tomo: i kwi vo? = and who is that?
lela: vo bebë nov da tiya da mi. = that is my aunt's new baby.
? who is ten years old? | Lela's cousin* | Lela's aunt | the baby
tomo: bebë pik! = the baby is small!
lela: ya. bebë nema. = yes. the baby sleeps.
? what is the baby doing? | sleeping* | eating | singing

module 1.5 | family traditions

story dinner at grandmother's
baba: vela, kitin! = hello, children!
ana: vela, baba! min ik vespa yama ka? = hello, grandmother! do we eat dinner here?
baba: ya! vespa gran. = yes! the dinner is big.
tomo: mi nami sipa. = I drink water.
? where do they eat dinner? | at grandmother's* | at school | in the park
baba: un vechë, dedo sago thela. = in the evening, grandfather tells a story.
ana: mi sagon da dedo liva! = I love grandfather's stories!
? who tells a story? | grandfather* | grandmother | Tomo

gold story the birthday
ama: vedey nasdey da ana! = today is Ana's birthday!
tomo: mi dara ra ana do. = I give a gift to Ana.
ana: shevan, tomo! = thank you, Tomo!
baba: mi torta fasa. = I make a cake.
? whose birthday is it? | Ana's* | Tomo's | grandmother's
dedo: min lir kun lira! = we sing a song together!
ana: mi mol fela. = I am very happy.
? who makes the cake? | grandmother* | Ana | Tomo

module 2 | food

story at the market
tomo: vela! mi aplon vola. = hello! I want apples.
ana: aplon rud ka? = red apples?
tomo: ya, rud. aplo kwanto kosta? = yes, red. how much does an apple cost?
ana: aplo ek mon kosta. = an apple costs one coin.
? how much does one apple cost? | one coin* | five coins | ten coins
tomo: mi pam aplo kopa. = I buy five apples.
ana: shevan! dano! = thank you! goodbye!
? how many apples does Tomo buy? | five* | one | two

module 2.5 | cooking and meals

story breakfast
ama: un matin, min pran yama. = in the morning, we eat breakfast.
tomo: mi yamik! = I am hungry!
ama: tu ovo vola ka? = do you want an egg?
tomo: ya, dor ovo! = yes, two eggs!
? what does Tomo want? | two eggs* | soup | a cake
ama: kafa amar. mi chay sipa. = coffee is bitter. I drink tea.
tomo: mi satë. shevan, ama! = I am full. thank you, mother!
? why doesn't mother drink coffee? | it is bitter* | it is cold | it is salty

gold story making a cake
ana: ama, mi torta fasa vola. = mother, I want to make a cake.
ama: bon! lista skri. = good! write a list.
ana: ovon, mikh i choko. = eggs, milk and chocolate.
ama: choko favo da tu! = chocolate is your favorite!
? what does Ana want to make? | a cake* | soup | bread
ana: torta sava i bon! = the cake is sweet and good!
ama: nun kukin limpa! = now clean the kitchen!
? what must Ana do at the end? | clean the kitchen* | buy milk | sleep

module 3 | home

story where is the cat?
ana: mish da mi kwe es? = where is my cat?
tomo: mish nër kama ka? = is the cat under the bed?
ana: nay. = no.
tomo: mish pos dur ka? = is the cat behind the door?
ana: nay! = no!
? what is Ana looking for? | her cat* | her dog | a book
tomo: mish gris sur kloza es! = the gray cat is on the closet!
ana: ya! mish da mi! = yes! my cat!
? where was the cat? | on the closet* | under the bed | behind the door

challenge story the new house
lela: kar nov da min gran! = our new house is big!
tomo: rum da tu kwe es? = where is your room?
lela: rum da mi supra es. kukin infra es. = my room is upstairs. the kitchen is downstairs.
tomo: kar gardo ha ka? = does the house have a garden?
lela: ya, gardo bela. galan multa es. = yes, the garden is beautiful. there are many flowers.
? where is Lela's room? | upstairs* | downstairs | in the garden
tomo: vesin da tu kwi? = who is your neighbor?
lela: vesin vet. sa vuf gran ha. = the neighbor is old. he has a big dog.
? what does the neighbor have? | a big dog* | a cat | a garden
