section 4

module 10 | shopping and money

story the sale
ana: vedey losur gran un rokem es! = today there is a big sale at the mall!
lela: mi dutlun nov vola. = I want a new jacket.
ana: ve dutlun siga ha. = this jacket has a discount.
lela: mi sa gani. kamaresh bon! = I try it on. the size is good!
debes: kel wobel o vobri? = with cash or a bank card?
lela: kel vobri. = with a bank card.
? what does Lela buy? | a jacket* | a dress | a hat
? how does Lela pay? | with a bank card* | with cash | she doesn't pay

challenge story if I were rich
tomo: sef tu teli esta, tu kwo kopave? = if you were rich, what would you buy?
ana: mi kar gran kopave. i tu? = I would buy a big house. and you?
tomo: mi zil ra human naske dove. = I would give money to poor people.
ana: tu mol bon! = you are very good!
? what would Ana buy? | a big house* | a car | a jacket
? what would Tomo do? | give money to poor people* | buy a house | save money

module 10.5 | the bank and the shop

story the wrong size
tomo: shun da mi kamaresh obru ha. = my shoes have the wrong size.
thun: tu fleno vola ka? = do you want a refund?
tomo: nay, mi shun gran vola. = no, I want big shoes.
thun: ve shun gran. = these shoes are big.
tomo: shevan! = thanks!
? what was the problem? | the wrong size* | the wrong color | they were broken
? does Tomo take a refund? | no, he takes bigger shoes* | yes | he takes nothing

gold story saving up
ana: mi zil zibe, orut mi biko kopa pot. = I save money so that I can buy a bike.
tomo: tu khosh zibeta ka? = did you save a lot?
ana: nay. biko karo. = no. the bike is expensive.
tomo: zalël losur chagi! = wait until the sale!
ana: ideya bon! mi chagiva. = good idea! I will wait.
? what does Ana want to buy? | a bike* | a phone | a jacket
? what is Tomo's advice? | wait until the sale* | borrow money | buy now

module 11 | technology

story the dead phone
lela: ronish da mi ne rabo! = my phone doesn't work!
tomo: zacha tedas ka? = is the battery empty?
lela: ya. leleleng da mi kwe es? = yes. where is my charger?
tomo: ik! = here!
lela: shevan! nun mi pasinzët ra ama mure pot. = thanks! now I can text a message to mother.
? what was wrong with the phone? | the battery was empty* | it broke | it had a virus
? who will Lela text? | her mother* | Tomo | her teacher

challenge story the robot
tomo: min shemde wabata! = we built a robot!
ana: shemde pasa pot ka? = can the robot walk?
tomo: ya, shom sozising samu ha. = yes, but the code has a bug.
ana: mi tu ayda. min samu retnoluva. = I'll help you. we will fix the bug.
tomo: nun shemde rabo! = now the robot works!
? what did they build? | a robot* | a computer | an app
? what was the problem? | a bug in the code* | no battery | a virus

module 11.5 | news and media

story fake news
ana: mi un reto ridëta ke mera ne es. = I read on the internet that the moon doesn't exist.
tomo: ve nova pelo! = this news is fake!
ana: mayë sa kika ka? = maybe it is real?
tomo: nay. tu nova kihi deba! = no. you must check the news!
? what did Ana read? | that the moon doesn't exist* | that the sun is cold | that cats can fly
? what does Tomo say? | the news is fake* | it's real | he doesn't know

gold story too much phone
ama: tomo, tu ronish khosh mengpa! = Tomo, you use the phone a lot!
tomo: mi puntilit luda. sa sishu! = I play a video game. it's exciting!
ama: ronish utho i teshi gol! = turn off the phone and go outside!
tomo: teshi, sol shina. mi fela! = outside, the sun shines. I am happy!
? what was Tomo doing? | playing a video game* | reading | sleeping
? what did mother say? | go outside* | go to sleep | do homework

module 12 | travel and culture

story the spicy dish
ana: mi thelor da ve nasho ne rava. = I don't understand the language of this country.
lela: mi thelan ranu kenë. = I know some words.
ana: kiro, menu selu! = please translate the menu!
lela: menu thela, mirin nirun luthu. = the menu says, the local dish is spicy.
ana: mi luthu ne goda! mi napa vola. = I don't like spicy! I want a salad.
? what does the menu say? | the local dish is spicy* | the dish is sweet | there is no food
? what does Ana want? | a salad* | the spicy dish | rice

challenge story the festival
tomo: vedey sopu gran es! = today there is a big festival!
lela: madok un vilë pasa. = the parade walks in the street.
tomo: kitin tëdlan chati. = the children hold lanterns.
lela: un noch, mutu esva! = at night, there will be fireworks!
tomo: mi ve nasho liva. human lëbë. = I love this country. the people are friendly.
? what will happen at night? | fireworks* | a parade | rain
? how are the people? | friendly* | angry | scary

module 12.5 | festivals and traditions

story the wedding
lela: tiya da mi posdey kevava! = my aunt will marry tomorrow!
ana: rizo kwe esva? = where will the wedding be?
lela: un kastë vet! = in an old castle!
ana: kastë? mesan lë viva! = a castle? ghosts live there!
lela: ve menisthi! ne kika. = that's a legend! not real.
? where will the wedding be? | in an old castle* | in a church | at the beach
? what does Ana think lives there? | ghosts* | dragons | a king

gold story coming home
ana: ish mi chiba, mi tu evuteva. = as soon as I arrive, I will call you.
ama: unu da tu kwu esta? = how was your journey?
ana: tamchin! mi noshsoman multa vidta. = interesting! I saw many cultures.
ama: tu ripë vayave ka? = would you travel again?
ana: ya! ve unu bonesh da tote da mi esta. = yes! this journey was the best of my life.
? how was the journey? | interesting* | boring | scary
? would Ana travel again? | yes* | no | maybe not
