section 2 | out in the world | the city, nature, school and work

module 4 | city and travel

lesson the city
new: urba, zor, rap, lenë
+ vilë | street | noun
+ oto | car | noun
+ tren | train | noun
urba gran i bela. = the city is big and beautiful.
oton multa un vilë es. = there are many cars on the street.
mi un urba viva. = I live in the city.
tren rap. = the train is fast.
oto da aba lenë. = father's car is slow.

lesson yesterday
new: -ta, ven, pasa, dos, kel
tip: past tense: add -ta to the verb. gol (go) becomes golta (went). kel means with, and also "by" for travel.
predey mi ra urba golta. = yesterday I went to the city.
min ra skol pasata. = we walked to school.
sa dos urba venta. = she came from the city.
tu kel tren golta ka? = did you go by train?
nay, mi kel oto golta. = no, I went by car.

lesson places
+ boteka | shop | noun
+ parka | park | noun
+ kafë | café | noun
+ muzë | museum | noun
+ hotel | hotel | noun
boteka nek parka es. = the shop is next to the park.
min un kafë seda. = we sit in the café.
mi ra muzë gol vola. = I want to go to the museum.
hotel kwe es? = where is the hotel?
predey san un parka ludata. = yesterday they played in the park.

lesson travel
new: nov, kwa
+ vaya | to travel | verb
+ nasho | country | noun
+ mapa | map | noun
+ bagë | bag | noun
mi vaya goda. = I like to travel.
san ra nasho nov vayata. = they traveled to a new country.
mapa da tu kwe es? = where is your map?
bagë da mi mol gran. = my bag is very big.
tu kwa vaya? = why do you travel?

lesson tomorrow
new: -va, bor
tip: future tense: add -va to the verb. gol becomes golva (will go).
posdey mi ra bor golva. = tomorrow I will go to the mountain.
min kel tren vayava. = we will travel by train.
sa ra hotel venva. = he will come to the hotel.
tu kwan golva? = when will you go?
mi ne golva. = I will not go.

module 4.5 | directions

lesson left and right
+ lev | left | adjective
+ dreta | right (not left) | adjective
+ latë | side | noun
+ rekto | straight ahead | adverb
boteka un latë lev es. = the shop is on the left side.
hotel un latë dreta es. = the hotel is on the right side.
rekto gol! = go straight ahead!
ra lev gol! = go left!
muzë rekto es. = the museum is straight ahead.

lesson asking the way
new: kweta, repa, ayda, hum
+ prok | near | adjective
+ dalë | far | adjective
mi hum kwetata. = I asked a person.
parka prok ka? = is the park near?
nay, parka dalë. = no, the park is far.
sa ne repata. = she did not answer.
kiro, mi ayda! = please help me!

lesson how far
new: lon
+ minut | minute | noun
+ or | hour, o'clock | noun
muzë dek minut dalë. = the museum is ten minutes away.
min ek or pasata. = we walked for one hour.
kwanto minut? = how many minutes?
zor lon. = the road is long.
tren dor or golva. = the train will go for two hours.

lesson getting lost
new: trova
+ perda | to lose, to get lost | verb
+ ripë | again | adverb
mi un urba perdata. = I got lost in the city.
mi hotel ne trovata. = I did not find the hotel.
vren da mi mi trovata. = my friend found me.
mapa ripë ridë! = read the map again!
min ne perdava. = we will not get lost.

lesson a trip
+ foto | photo | noun
+ pelag | sea | noun
min ra pelag vayata. = we traveled to the sea.
mi foton multa fasata. = I took many photos.
pelag blu i bela. = the sea is blue and beautiful.
posdey min ra kar golva. = tomorrow we will go home.
tu foton da mi goda ka? = do you like my photos?

module 5 | nature and weather

lesson the sky
new: sol, mera, stel, vin
+ nuba | cloud | noun
sol un vin es. = the sun is in the sky.
un noch, stelan multa es. = at night, there are many stars.
mera bela. = the moon is beautiful.
nuban gris. = the clouds are gray.
vedey nuba ne es. = there is no cloud today.

lesson weather
new: khal, forë
+ klima | weather | noun
+ pluva | to rain | verb
+ neva | to snow | verb
tip: weather verbs have no subject: "pluva" alone means "it rains".
klima vedey kwu? = how is the weather today?
vedey pluva. = it rains today.
predey nevata. = yesterday it snowed.
khal mol forë. = the wind is very strong.
posdey kal esva. = tomorrow it will be hot.

lesson nature
new: filo, gala, ruma, tera
+ lago | lake | noun
+ silva | forest | noun
silva talan multa ha. = the forest has many trees.
ruma ra lago gol. = the river goes to the lake.
filon verdë. = the leaves are green.
gala rud nek tal es. = a red flower is next to the tree.
bor gran i alba. = the mountain is big and white.

lesson bigger and best
new: -or, -r, -esh, -sh
+ dë | than | particle
tip: add -or (-r after a vowel) for "more": gran, granor. add -esh (-sh after a vowel) for "most": granesh. "than" is dë.
bor granor dë kar. = the mountain is bigger than the house.
ruma lonor dë zor. = the river is longer than the road.
ve gala belar dë vo. = this flower is more beautiful than that one.
sol granesh. = the sun is the biggest.
ve lago belash. = this lake is the most beautiful.

lesson animals
new: pita, glob
+ kaval | horse | noun
+ uso | bear | noun
+ vulka | wolf | noun
+ ala | to fly | verb
pitan un vin ala. = the birds fly in the sky.
glob un nami viva. = the fish lives in the water.
kaval rapor dë vuf. = the horse is faster than the dog.
uso un silva viva. = the bear lives in the forest.
vulka granor dë mish. = the wolf is bigger than the cat.

module 5.5 | seasons and animals

lesson seasons
new: yer
+ sezon | season | noun
+ primë | spring | noun
+ somër | summer | noun
+ otno | autumn | noun
+ hiver | winter | noun
un hiver, neva. = it snows in winter.
un somër, min ra pelag gol. = in summer, we go to the sea.
otno filon rud ha. = autumn has red leaves.
primë bela. = spring is beautiful.
yer kav sezon ha. = a year has four seasons.

lesson on the farm
new: kura
+ ferma | farm | noun
+ kova | cow | noun
+ pulo | chicken | noun
+ kampo | field | noun
dedo un ferma viva. = grandfather lives on a farm.
kova mikh do. = the cow gives milk.
pulon ovon do. = the chickens give eggs.
kavalan un kampo kura. = the horses run in the field.
ferma da dedo gran. = grandfather's farm is big.

lesson how you do it
new: -mo
tip: add -mo to an adjective to say how something is done: rap (fast), rapmo (quickly).
kaval rapmo kura. = the horse runs quickly.
uso lenëmo pasa. = the bear walks slowly.
pita belamo lira. = the bird sings beautifully.
tu bonmo thela. = you speak well.
kiti felamo ludata. = the child played happily.

lesson caring for nature
+ garda | to protect | verb
+ resto | trash | noun
+ puro | clean | adjective
+ mudë | dirty | adjective
+ lasa | to leave, to let | verb
min tera garda. = we protect the earth.
ruma puro. = the river is clean.
lago mudë. = the lake is dirty.
resto un silva ne lasa! = do not leave trash in the forest!
posdey min ruma puro fasava. = tomorrow we will make the river clean.

lesson a day outside
+ piknik | picnic | noun
+ shina | to shine | verb
vedey sol shina. = today the sun is shining.
min piknik un parka fasata. = we had a picnic in the park.
kitin felamo ludata. = the children played happily.
un vechë, pluvata. = in the evening, it rained.
ve dey belash esta. = this was the most beautiful day.

module 6 | school and work

lesson subjects
new: khar, lekh
+ matë | math | noun
+ histo | history | noun
+ muzik | music | noun
+ arta | art | noun
mi matë lerna. = I study math.
tu histo goda ka? = do you like history?
arta mol bela. = art is very beautiful.
matë khar. = math is hard.
muzik lekh. = music is easy.

lesson can, must, want
+ pot | can | verb
+ deba | must, to have to | verb
tip: can (pot), must (deba) and want (vola) come after the main verb, and they take the -ta or -va: "mi gol potta", I could go.
mi lira pot. = I can sing.
tu matë lerna deba. = you must study math.
sa nema vola. = she wants to sleep.
mi vendëri thela pot. = I can speak Venderian.
predey mi ne gol potta. = yesterday I could not go.

lesson the classroom
+ klasë | class, classroom | noun
+ pena | pen | noun
+ papir | paper | noun
+ panël | board | noun
mestro sur panël skri. = the teacher writes on the board.
mi pena i papir ha. = I have a pen and paper.
klasë da min gran. = our class is big.
kiro, kitën da tun apra! = please open your books!
papir ne ik es. = the paper is not here.

lesson first, second, third
new: -i
tip: add -i to a number for first, second...: ek, eki. clock time is or + the number: "un or okh", at eight o'clock.
ve dey eki da skol. = this is the first day of school.
mi kitë tisi ridë. = I read the third book.
skol un or okh apra. = school opens at eight o'clock.
or kwo? = what time is it?
or dek. = it is ten o'clock.

lesson homework
+ tasko | homework, task | noun
+ fini | to finish | verb
+ prova | test | noun
mi tasko fasa deba. = I have to do homework.
tu tasko finita ka? = did you finish the homework?
posdey prova esva. = tomorrow there will be a test.
prova khar esta. = the test was hard.
mi tasko apre vespa finiva. = I will finish the homework after dinner.

module 6.5 | time and plans

lesson jobs
new: -ri
+ medik | doctor | noun
tip: add -ri to a verb for the person who does it: lira (sing), lirari (singer).
mi lirari esva. = I will be a singer.
aba da mi kukari. = my father is a cook.
medik human ayda. = the doctor helps people.
tu kwo rab ha? = what job do you have?
ama da mi medik. = my mother is a doctor.

lesson days of the week
+ vek | week | noun
tip: the days are numbered: dey eki (first day) is monday, dey sibi (seventh day) is sunday.
vek sib dey ha. = a week has seven days.
dey eki, mi skol gol. = on Monday, I go to school.
dey pami, min festa fasava. = on Friday, we will have a party.
dey sibi, mi nema. = on Sunday, I sleep.
vedey dey kwo? = what day is it today?

lesson plans
new: penka
+ plan | plan | noun
+ mesë | month | noun
+ pros | next | adjective
+ pasë | last (before this one) | adjective
mi plan ha. = I have a plan.
vek pros, min vayava. = next week, we will travel.
mesë pasë, mi multamo rabota. = last month, I worked a lot.
plan da tu kwo? = what is your plan?
yer pros, mi ra skol nov golva. = next year, I will go to a new school.

lesson busy and free
new: kron
+ okupa | busy | adjective
+ libër | free (not busy) | adjective
mi vedey okupa. = I am busy today.
tu posdey libër ka? = are you free tomorrow?
mi kron ne ha. = I do not have time.
sa kron multa ha. = she has a lot of time.
apre rab, mi libër. = after work, I am free.

lesson a busy week
tip: ve (this) and vo (that) go before the noun, like numbers: ve vek, this week.
dey eki, mi prova ha. = on Monday, I have a test.
dey dori, mi muzik lerna. = on Tuesday, I study music.
dey tisi, mi kel vren ludava. = on Wednesday, I will play with a friend.
ve vek, mi mol okupa. = this week, I am very busy.
shom dey sibi, mi libër. = but on Sunday, I am free.
