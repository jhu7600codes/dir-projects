section 4 | living your life | money, technology, travel and culture

module 10 | shopping and money

lesson at the mall
new: rokem, losur, siga, posmong, kamaresh
min ra rokem golta. = we went to the mall.
vedey losur gran es. = today there is a big sale.
ve kamis siga ha. = this shirt has a discount.
posmong kwo? = what is the price?
kamaresh da tu kwo? = what is your size?

lesson trying things on
new: gani, venne, dutlun, boni, loracho
mi ve dutlun gani vola. = I want to try on this jacket.
venne bela, shom karo. = the dress is beautiful but expensive.
boni pik. = the coat is small.
tu loracho gani ka? = are you trying on a hat?
ve kamaresh bonor. = this size is better.

lesson paying
new: wobel, vobri, debes, kudlas, adeku
mi kel vobri paga. = I pay with a bank card.
tu wobel ha ka? = do you have cash?
debes kudlas ra mi dota. = the cashier gave me the receipt.
adeku da mi kwe es? = where is my wallet?
mi adeku perdata! = I lost my wallet!

lesson shopping online
new: ose, kanna, khadish, athosh, chiba
mi shun ose kannata. = I ordered shoes online.
khadish posdey chibava. = the delivery will arrive tomorrow.
athosh gran ik es. = a big package is here.
athosh da mi ne chibata. = my package did not arrive.
mi ose kopa goda. = I like to buy online.

lesson would
new: -ve
tip: add -ve to a verb for "would": kopa (buy), kopave (would buy).
mi ve oto kopave. = I would buy this car.
tu kwo kopave? = what would you buy?
sa ne golve. = she would not go.
mi ra pelag golve. = I would go to the sea.
min aydave. = we would help.

lesson if I were rich
new: teli, naske, sito, zibe
tip: sef with the past, then -ve: "sef mi teli esta, mi kopave", if I were rich, I would buy.
sef mi teli esta, mi kar gran kopave. = if I were rich, I would buy a big house.
sef tu zil hata, tu kwo fasave? = if you had money, what would you do?
mi zil zibe. = I save up money.
sito da mi pik. = my savings are small.
hum naske zil ne ha. = a poor person does not have money.

lesson jobs and money
new: thalvesas, pulbo, mowo, yatorët, tanë, kasha
aba zil multa pulbo. = father earns a lot of money.
thalvesas da ama bon. = mother's salary is good.
min mowo paga deba. = we must pay taxes.
tanë da tiyo gran. = my uncle's company is big.
mi yatorët kasha ha vola. = I want to have my own business.

lesson borrowing
new: gifa, tomi, evi, yeshkli, inking
kiro, zil ra mi tomi. = please lend me money.
mi pena da tu gifa pot ka? = can I borrow your pen?
tu dek mon ra mi evi. = you owe me ten coins.
inking mal. = debt is bad.
tanë yeshkli gran ha. = the company has a big loan.

lesson spending wisely
new: thaknodo, lesi, wangmile, rekame, la, gras
mi zil multa thaknodota. = I spent a lot of money.
zil ne lesi! = do not waste money!
min ve oto wangmile ne pot. = we cannot afford this car.
ve shi rekame ka? = is this thing necessary?
la thaknodo, gras zibe! = spend less, save more!

lesson market day
new: kihi
losur da bazar mol bon esta. = the market's sale was very good.
mi kamis i shun kopata. = I bought a shirt and shoes.
posmong ne karo esta. = the price was not expensive.
mi kudlas ripë kihi. = I check the receipt again.
posdey mi ripë kopava. = tomorrow I will buy again.

module 10.5 | the bank and the shop

lesson the bank
new: lozun, chagi
lozun nek boteka es. = the bank is next to the shop.
mi un lozun chagita. = I waited in the bank.
sito da mi un lozun es. = my savings are in the bank.
lozun vobri nov ra mi dota. = the bank gave me a new bank card.
lozun un or pam klu. = the bank closes at five o'clock.

lesson selling things
new: puba, tanset, thun, eda, glarol, kulom
mi biko vet pubata. = I sold my old bike.
thun aplon puba. = the seller sells apples.
tansetan multa ven. = many customers come.
ve glarol kulom. = this brand is popular.
eda zil ra thun do. = the buyer gives money to the seller.

lesson quality
new: pedosshi, durlavlu, tush, ham, goret
pedosshi da ve kamis mal. = the quality of this shirt is bad.
aplon durlavlu. = the apples are fresh.
ve banan tush! = this banana is rotten!
torta da baba ham. = grandmother's cake is delicious.
karnë goret ne yama! = do not eat raw meat!

lesson returns
new: fleno, veksabe, obru, sutha
shun kamaresh obru ha. = the shoes have the wrong size.
mi shun ra boteka veksabeta. = I returned the shoes to the shop.
mi fleno vola. = I want a refund.
boteka fleno suthata. = the shop refused the refund.
mi angra esta. = I was angry.

lesson gift shopping
new: yile, kimola, zani
mi dara ra ama zani deba. = I have to choose a gift for mother.
yile ik prok es. = the gift shop is near here.
ama galan kimola. = mother prefers flowers.
mi buket kopava. = I will buy a bouquet.
ama mol fela esva. = mother will be very happy.

lesson while
new: nirfung
tip: nirfung (while) works like kwan: the while part first, then the rest.
nirfung mi kopa, vren chagi. = while I shop, my friend waits.
nirfung ama kuka, aba platon limpa. = while mother cooks, father cleans the plates.
nirfung min un rokem esta, pluvata. = while we were at the mall, it rained.
nirfung tu nema, mi rabo. = while you sleep, I work.
nirfung mi pasa, mi muzik ota. = while I walk, I listen to music.

lesson until
new: zalël, gëses
tip: zalël (until) goes before a time, or starts its own part like nirfung.
zalël tu venva, mi chagiva. = until you come, I will wait.
mi zalël or dek rabo. = I work until ten o'clock.
zalël vechë, min un parka ludata. = until the evening, we played in the park.
zalël losur, mi ne kopava. = until the sale, I will not buy.
boteka zalël noch gëses. = the shop is open until night.

lesson the shopping basket
new: lun, pepa, pushkuk, kasro, antunor, guplu, keszu, vashingvi
mi pepa da mikh vola. = I want a bottle of milk.
lun da guplu sur tabla es. = a jar of jam is on the table.
pushkuk da keszu kopa! = buy a box of cookies!
ama kasro i antunor kopata. = mother bought flour and sugar.
torta kasro i antunor vashingvi. = a cake needs flour and sugar.

lesson so that
new: orut, kisni, nidi, pisu
tip: orut (so that) says why: "mi zibe, orut mi kopa pot", I save so that I can buy.
mi zil zibe, orut mi biko kopa pot. = I save money so that I can buy a bike.
mi kisni, orut mi un prova nidi. = I study so that I succeed in the test.
ama lista skri, orut sa ne oblida. = mother writes a list so that she does not forget.
min pisu, orut min ne tarda. = we hurry so that we are not late.
aba trena, orut sa forër es. = father trains so that he is stronger.

lesson a smart shopper
new: kidusok, damo, khosh
mi zil kidusok thaknodo. = I spend money carefully.
posmongan damo! = compare the prices!
sef posmong karo, zalël losur chagi! = if the price is expensive, wait until the sale!
mi khosh zibeta. = I saved a lot.
nun mi sito gran ha. = now I have big savings.

module 11 | technology

lesson my phone
new: ronish, sori, zacha, leleleng, shitle, tedas
mi ronish nov ha. = I have a new phone.
zacha da ronish da mi tedas. = my phone's battery is empty.
leleleng da mi kwe es? = where is my charger?
ronish shitle! = turn on the phone!
sori da sesa karo. = my sister's smartphone is expensive.

lesson messages
new: pasinzët, mure, vakeng, kurdlu
mi pasinzët ra vren mureta. = I texted a message to my friend.
mi vakeng nov kurdluta. = I received a new email.
tu pasinzët da mi ridëta ka? = did you read my message?
mi vakeng ra mestro mandava. = I will send an email to the teacher.
vren ne repata. = my friend did not answer.

lesson websites and apps
new: kumin, benësh, lanse, zushoshya, pashashta, kitshuk
mi kumin nov trovata. = I found a new website.
ve benësh mol kitshuk. = this app is very useful.
lanse da tu ne thela! = do not tell your password!
mi un reto zushoshya. = I search on the internet.
sur ve pashashta! = click on this!

lesson social media
new: kekë, maku, walën, dika, kuvu, yepepa
sesa da mi kekë mol goda. = my sister likes social media a lot.
mi foto nov dikata. = I posted a new photo.
tu makun multa ha ka? = do you have many followers?
walën da tu bon esta. = your comment was good.
kuvu da mi yepepa. = my profile is private.

lesson videos
new: rami, klaba, tikkin, dine, murwa, shubu
mi video nov dineta. = I filmed a new video.
tikkin da mi dek maku ha. = my channel has ten followers.
lirari vedey klabava. = the singer will stream today.
min rami kun shubuta. = we watched the stream together.
mi lir da mi murwa vola. = I want to record my song.

lesson files
new: noshmas, giwa, oyon, dashuti, rini, panhong, tiku
mi giwa un oyon tiku. = I put the file in the folder.
giwa da mi ne dashuti! = do not delete my file!
mi benësh nov rinita. = I installed a new app.
noshmas da aba vet i lenë. = father's laptop is old and slow.
kompu panhong vola. = the computer wants an update.

lesson when tech breaks
new: samu, brobung, retnolu, velle, shupi
kompu da mi brobung ha. = my computer has a virus.
ronish da mi velleta. = my phone broke.
teknik ronish retnoluta. = the engineer fixed the phone.
benësh samu multa ha. = the app has many bugs.
ripë shupi! = try again!

lesson coding
new: sozising, tudapa, kemi, shemde, waba
mi tudapa lerna. = I am learning to code.
kemi sozising skri. = the programmer writes code.
sozising da mi rabo! = my code works!
min shemde pik wabata. = we built a small robot.
shemde da min sur tabla pasa. = our robot walks on the table.

lesson staying safe online
new: rede, rimos, tline, hoshzir
rede vala. = privacy is important.
rimos kumin tlineta. = a hacker hacked the website.
lanse lon i khar fasa! = make a long and hard password!
sef lanse da tu lekh, rimos tu tline pot. = if your password is easy, a hacker can hack you.
mi un reto hoshzir senta. = I feel safe on the internet.

lesson a day online
new: utho
un matin, mi pasinzëtan ridë. = in the morning, I read messages.
un medey, mi video shubu. = at lunch, I watch a video.
un vechë, mi foto dika. = in the evening, I post a photo.
shom un noch, ronish utho! = but at night, turn off the phone!
ronish utho i nema. = turn off the phone and sleep.

module 11.5 | news and media

lesson television and papers
new: savosh, rekul, lushtumon, hafi
aba lushtumon dey tot ridë. = father reads the newspaper every day.
savosh ne bon. = the television is not good.
hafi ve sago skrita. = a journalist wrote this story.
ama rekul kopata. = mother bought a magazine.
min un vechë savosh shubu. = we watch television in the evening.

lesson radio and headphones
new: monhachum, botero, damfo, nisimi, odo
mi damfo nov ha. = I have new headphones.
mi muzik kel damfo nisimi. = I listen to music with headphones.
dedo monhachum vet ha. = grandfather has an old radio.
botero mol odo. = the speaker is very loud.
un oto, min monhachum nisimi. = in the car, we listen to the radio.

lesson rumors
new: woye, mulal, kika, pelo, lute
ve woye ne ver. = this rumor is not true.
nova pelo multa un reto es. = there is a lot of fake news on the internet.
nova ripë kihi! = check the news again!
ve foto kika ka? = is this photo real?
mulal da mi lute. = my opinion is different.

lesson although
new: ming, fatiga
tip: ming (although) is another two-part word: the although part first.
ming pluva, min ra parka golva. = although it rains, we will go to the park.
ming sa fatiga, sa rabo. = although she is tired, she works.
ming ve ronish karo, mi sa kopava. = although this phone is expensive, I will buy it.
ming mi kisnita, prova khar esta. = although I studied, the test was hard.
ming tu pik, tu forë. = although you are small, you are strong.

lesson unless
new: fan, kaso
tip: fan (unless) also starts its own part.
fan tu pisu, tu tarda esva. = unless you hurry, you will be late.
fan pluva, min sur playa luda. = unless it rains, we play on the beach.
fan tu lanse kenë, tu kaso ne pot. = unless you know the password, you cannot enter.
fan ronish zacha ha, sa ne rabo. = unless the phone has battery, it does not work.
mi ne golva, fan tu golva. = I will not go unless you go.

lesson games online
new: puntilit, lutlozon, bëbash, kava, sishu
min puntilit kun luda. = we play a video game together.
tu ekipa da min kava ka? = will you join our team?
bëbash da mi granor dë bëbash da tu. = my score is bigger than your score.
lutlozonan multa un reto es. = there are many players on the internet.
ve ludo mol sishu. = this game is very exciting.

lesson selfies
new: shasotlush, mukpra, laro, poshna, vater
mi shasotlush fasata. = I took a selfie.
laro! = smile!
foton da tu ra mi poshna! = show me your photos!
mukpra foton bela fasa. = the photographer takes beautiful photos.
shasotlush da sesa vater. = my sister's selfie is funny.

lesson whether
new: riblin, masoplo
tip: riblin (whether) comes after verbs like know, ask and wonder, like ke does.
mi ne kenë riblin sa venva. = I do not know whether she will come.
mi kweta riblin tu libër. = I ask whether you are free.
sa ne kenë riblin ve ver. = she does not know whether this is true.
tu kenë riblin bus tarda ka? = do you know whether the bus is late?
mi masoplo riblin mi gol deba. = I wonder whether I must go.

lesson too much screen
new: mengpa, plë, resdi, etera, teshi
tu ronish khosh mengpa. = you use the phone a lot.
visan da tu plë vola. = your eyes want rest.
ronish utho i resdi! = turn off the phone and relax!
teshi gol i etera! = go outside and breathe!
la ronish, gras sol! = less phone, more sun!

lesson tech and me
new: vuvo
mi ronish, noshmas i damfo ha. = I have a phone, a laptop and headphones.
reto rekame, shom ne vuvo. = the internet is necessary, but it is not everything.
mi penka ke kompun kitshuk. = I think that computers are useful.
ming mi kekë goda, mi vrenan kika kimola. = although I like social media, I prefer real friends.
ronish utho i vren da tu vizita! = turn off the phone and visit your friend!

module 12 | travel and culture

lesson passports
new: fimeno, opit, onbat, lëli, zime, notë, tënanma
mi fimeno nov ha. = I have a new passport.
tu opit vashingvi ka? = do you need a visa?
min onbat tënanmata. = we crossed the border.
un nasho notë, mi lëli. = in a foreign country, I am a foreigner.
zime da nasho da min ik es. = our country's embassy is here.

lesson the flight
new: asovu, sone, vazar, redu, lete
asovu da min tarda. = our flight is late.
sone da mi gran i lete. = my luggage is big and heavy.
vazar da mi nek vindë es. = my seat is next to the window.
avyon un or tis chibava. = the airplane will arrive at three o'clock.
redu kwe es? = where is the exit?

lesson new languages
new: vadogus, dengfa, selu, rava, sinang, ranu
mi thelor da ve nasho ne rava. = I do not understand the language of this country.
kiro, ve selu! = please translate this!
vadogus da tu bela. = your accent is beautiful.
ik human dengfa sinang thela. = here people speak another dialect.
mi thelan ranu kenë. = I know some words.

lesson local food
new: mirin, nirun, luthu, ler, napa, balung, avim

ve mirin luthu. = this dish is spicy.
mi balung kel karnë yama. = I eat rice with meat.
avim ler. = the lemon is sour.
mi napa kimola. = I prefer salad.

lesson customs
new: podo, lolivo, garar, noshsom, shelmi, hotri

lolivo da ve nasho vet. = the tradition of this country is old.
min noshsom nirun shelmi deba. = we must respect the local culture.
hotri garar mol bela. = the traditional costume is very beautiful.
mi noshsoman multa lerna vola. = I want to learn many cultures.

lesson greetings
new: tildli, devo, ashir, vichi, yovi
ik human kel khan tildli. = here people greet with the hand.
ashir! = welcome!
human da ve urba mi devota. = the people of this city welcomed me.
tu yovi es deba. = you must be polite.
mi tot vichita. = I thanked everyone.

lesson festivals
new: sopu, madok, mutu, tëdla, dolme, chati
vedey sopu gran es. = today there is a big festival.
madok un vilë pasa. = the parade walks in the street.
un noch, mutu bela esta. = at night, the fireworks were beautiful.
kitin tëdlan chati. = the children hold lanterns.
tot kun dolme. = everyone celebrates together.

lesson exploring
new: usmi, dlima, unu, sorus, kutim, kaso
min kutim usmita. = we explored the island.
unu lon, shom bela esta. = the journey was long but beautiful.
sorus urba ra min poshnata. = the guide showed us the city.
ve dlima sishu esta! = this adventure was exciting!
mi dlima nov vola. = I want a new adventure.

lesson wild places
new: puvu, darite, lada, letruk, owon, odrek, tate
puvu kal i darite. = the desert is hot and dry.
lada letrukan multa ha. = the jungle has many animals.
owon gran i odo. = the waterfall is big and loud.
min un odrek kasota. = we entered a cave.
tate granor dë pelag. = the ocean is bigger than the sea.

lesson a postcard
new: lëbë
vela dos kutim! = hello from the island!
klima kal i sol shina. = the weather is hot and the sun shines.
mi mirinan nov yamata. = I ate new dishes.
human lëbë. = the people are friendly.
mi tu ne oblida! = I do not forget you!

module 12.5 | festivals and traditions

lesson a wedding
new: rizo, keva, eku, dema
tiya da mi kevava. = my aunt will get married.
rizo un primë esva. = the wedding will be in spring.
eku bela esta. = the ceremony was beautiful.
apre eku, dema gran esta. = after the ceremony, there was a big feast.
tot dansata. = everyone danced.

lesson national day
new: bilifi, mukhash, vlëmen, nuten
bilifi da nasho da min blu i alba. = our country's flag is blue and white.
human mukhash lira. = the people sing the anthem.
un vlëmen, bilifin multa es. = in town, there are many flags.
mi nuten da ve nasho. = I am a citizen of this country.
vedey dey vala da nasho. = today is an important day of the country.

lesson costumes
new: novuk, wigo, ulet, venesh
kitin hotrin vesta. = the children put on costumes.
novuk da tu venesh! = your mask is scary!
mi kel hotri da drako wigota. = I dressed in a dragon costume.
hotri da bro ulet. = my brother's costume is weird.
tot ridata. = everyone laughed.

lesson legends
new: menisthi, semulom, mesa, poshë, thunpo, lebis
menisthi thunpo lebis ve urba thela. = an ancient legend tells about this city.
dedo semuloman thela. = grandfather tells myths.
tu mesan kreda ka? = do you believe in ghosts?
poshë un odrek viva. = a monster lives in the cave.
ve sago ne kika. = this story is not real.

lesson holiday treats
new: leklilë, abam, yifetil, tëkire
baba leklilë tëkire. = grandmother bakes a pie.
kitin keszun yama. = the children eat cookies.
un somër, min abam yama. = in summer, we eat ice cream.
leklilë kel yifetil mol ham. = pie with cream is very delicious.
mi keszun ra vesin dota. = I gave cookies to the neighbor.

lesson faith and respect
new: kibi, navo, cholkhoro, duva, lamor
un ve urba, navo i cholkhoro es. = in this city, there is a church and a temple.
human kibin lute ha. = people have different religions.
min lamor ra tot do deba. = we must give respect to everyone.
dedo duva. = grandfather is religious.
cholkhoro vet i silen. = the temple is old and quiet.

lesson gifts and thanks
new: glusa, yone, ika, rugetë
sa dara ra mi glusata. = she offered me a gift.
mi dara yoneta. = I accepted the gift.
mi mol ika. = I am very grateful.
shevan, tu mol rugetë! = thank you, you are very generous!
mi tu vichi. = I thank you.

lesson as soon as
new: ish, evute, raluyo
tip: ish (as soon as) starts its own part too.
ish mi chiba, mi tu evuteva. = as soon as I arrive, I will call you.
ish sol shina, min golva. = as soon as the sun shines, we will go.
ish festa raluyo, tot dansa. = as soon as the party starts, everyone dances.
ish tu tasko fini, tu luda pot. = as soon as you finish the homework, you can play.
ish dur gëses, kitin kura. = as soon as the door is open, the children run.

lesson travel memories
new: ara, tote
mi dlima da mi rekorda. = I remember my adventure.
foton memon bela do. = photos give beautiful memories.
un ara, min multamo vayata. = in the past, we traveled a lot.
mi ra kutim ripë golve. = I would go to the island again.
ve unu bonesh da tote da mi esta. = this journey was the best of my life.

lesson home again
new: tamchin
un fin, min ra kar tornata. = in the end, we came back home.
nasho da mi bonesh. = my country is the best.
shom mi nashon sinang liva. = but I love other countries.
noshsoman multa mol tamchin. = many cultures are very interesting.
yer pros, min ripë vayava! = next year, we will travel again!
